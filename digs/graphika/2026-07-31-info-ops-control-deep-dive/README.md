## Bottom line

Graphika is not merely a passive “misinformation research” shop. Official DoD award abstracts describe a stack that maps target communities, predicts narrative virality, models online-to-offline behavior, identifies language and calls to action, measures engagement, supports counter-messaging, and enables earlier intervention.

Calling that an **information-operations intelligence and intervention-support system** is directly supported by the records. Calling it **information control** is a reasonable functional interpretation, but it remains an analytic label: the reviewed records do not show Graphika possessing unilateral censorship authority.

## Capability chain

1. **Map the information environment:** identify communities, influential segments, narrative flow, and cyber-social terrain.
2. **Detect operations:** coordinated inauthentic behavior, influence campaigns, AI-driven accounts, and emerging narrative shifts.
3. **Forecast spread:** Contagion Monitor predicts virality and critical-mass outbreaks.
4. **Connect online influence to offline behavior:** DoD abstracts explicitly discuss riots, in-person voting, target-population behavior, and calls to action.
5. **Select intervention points:** identify communities and linguistic markers relevant to planners and operators.
6. **Shape response:** awards describe counter-message planning, maintaining information advantage, measuring engagement effectiveness, and earlier potential intervention.

## Assessment

**Supported:** Graphika provides information-environment surveillance, influence detection, virality forecasting, target-community analysis, online-to-offline causal inference, counter-message planning, and intervention support.

**Inference:** This stack can function as information control infrastructure when its outputs are used by military planners, governments, or platforms to suppress, redirect, or disrupt narratives.

**Not established:** direct Graphika ownership or direction by Peter Thiel; direct DoD financing of the Virality Project; unilateral Graphika authority over platform moderation.
