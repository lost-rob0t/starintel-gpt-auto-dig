## Bottom line

Graphika is not merely a passive “misinformation research” shop. Official DoD award abstracts describe a stack that maps target communities, predicts narrative virality, models online-to-offline behavior, identifies language and calls to action, measures engagement, supports counter-messaging, and enables earlier intervention.

The depth-3 pass also establishes Graphika's place in the **content-moderation pipeline**. It usually did not exercise the platform's final ban/removal authority. It produced intelligence, participated in ticketing and flagging coalitions, identified accounts and narratives, and supported or advocated platform intervention.

See [`censorship-and-awards-depth-3.md`](./censorship-and-awards-depth-3.md) for the case ledger and reconciled award table.

## Capability chain

1. **Map the information environment:** identify communities, influential segments, narrative flow, and cyber-social terrain.
2. **Detect operations:** coordinated inauthentic behavior, influence campaigns, AI-driven accounts, and emerging narrative shifts.
3. **Forecast spread:** Contagion Monitor predicts virality and critical-mass outbreaks.
4. **Connect online influence to offline behavior:** award abstracts explicitly discuss riots, in-person voting, target-population behavior, and calls to action.
5. **Select intervention points:** identify communities and linguistic markers relevant to planners and operators.
6. **Shape response:** counter-message planning, maintaining information advantage, measuring engagement effectiveness, and earlier intervention.
7. **Measure results:** assess target-audience engagement and behavioral changes over time.
