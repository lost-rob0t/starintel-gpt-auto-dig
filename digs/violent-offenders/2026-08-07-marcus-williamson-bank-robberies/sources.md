## Ohio statutory sentencing baseline

- Ohio Revised Code Chapter 2929 / **R.C. 2929.11 and 2929.13** — current official Ohio Laws text. R.C. 2929.11 requires felony sentences to be commensurate with seriousness and consistent with sentences for similar crimes by similar offenders. R.C. 2929.13(D)(1) creates a presumption in favor of prison for F1/F2 felonies; R.C. 2929.13(D)(2) permits community control only after the court makes both required findings.  
  https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/chapter-2929
- Ohio Criminal Sentencing Commission / Supreme Court of Ohio — **Felony Sentencing Quick Reference Guide**, section on presumptive prison terms.  
  https://www.supremecourt.ohio.gov/Boards/Sentencing/resources/judPractitioner/felonyQuickRef.pdf

## Franklin County / Tenth District comparators

### State v. Gilbert — strongest serial-robbery comparator located

- **State v. Sylvete P. Gilbert**, Tenth District Court of Appeals, No. 12AP-142, Franklin County C.P. No. 11CR-58 (Nov. 29, 2012).  
  https://www.casemine.com/judgement/us/5914ec0cadd7b04934947949
- Gilbert was accused in 12 robberies and convicted for involvement in 11: **9 F2 + 2 F3 robbery convictions**.
- The robber approached bank/hotel employees, claimed to have a gun, demanded cash, and used disguises.
- Sentence: **51 years incarceration**, based on consecutive five-year F2 terms and three-year F3 terms.
- The Tenth District affirmed and emphasized that Gilbert committed robbery after robbery with careful preparation and that the sheer number warranted significant incarceration.
- Material differences from Williamson: Gilbert committed 11 vs. Williamson's 9 Ohio bank robberies, had 9 rather than 5 F2 counts, was on community control at the time, went to trial, and was sentenced under an older statutory regime.

### Raylin Jarrett — guilty plea, serial robberies

- Franklin County Prosecutor's Office — **Raylin Jarrett sentenced to 13 years in prison after pleading guilty to multiple robberies in Franklin County**, March 30, 2023.  
  https://prosecutor.franklincountyohio.gov/press-releases/1560
- Plea: aggravated robbery with a three-year firearm specification plus **12 robbery counts**.
- Sentence: **13 years prison**.
- Material difference: actual firearm specification/aggravated robbery makes Jarrett's legal conduct more severe than Williamson's pleaded counts.

### State v. Cunningham — Columbus bank robberies with threats

- **State v. Mario H. Cunningham**, 2024-Ohio-2032, Tenth District No. 21AP-470, Franklin County C.P. No. 18CR-5708.  
  https://law.justia.com/cases/ohio/tenth-district-court-of-appeals/2024/21ap-470.html
- Conduct: three Columbus bank robberies; jury convicted Cunningham of four **F2 robbery counts** with repeat-violent-offender specifications.
- Threat conduct included a fake bomb threat, threats to shoot people, and telling a teller he had a gun while motioning as though armed.
- Sentence: **24 years prison**.
- Material differences: RVO specifications, jury trial, and aggravating threat history make Cunningham a harsher case than Williamson in important respects.
