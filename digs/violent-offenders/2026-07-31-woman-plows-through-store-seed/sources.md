## Classification rules

- A video upload is a provenance artifact and investigative lead.
- Titles, descriptions, narration, captions, and edited footage remain attributed claims until independently corroborated.
- Law-enforcement releases can establish what an agency alleges, but not guilt.
- Arrest and booking records establish custody events, not conviction.
- Filed complaints, indictments, and charging documents establish formal allegations.
- Court dockets, judgments, verdicts, plea records, and sentencing entries establish adjudication and disposition.
- News reports may corroborate identity and chronology but should not replace primary legal records when those records are available.
