## The publication gate

The dataset rule is simple: a named person enters the public dataset only when identity is supported by an official source or by two independent reliable public sources agreeing on the person, event, and jurisdiction.

**All 10 cleared that gate.** Three cases initially needed a second pass:

- **Angel Javier Rodriguez** - KPRC/Click2Houston, ABC13 Houston, Houston Chronicle.
- **Cody Prince** - KPRC/Click2Houston, ABC13 Houston.
- **Jonathan Castelan** - NBC Los Angeles, CBS Los Angeles, FOX 11 Los Angeles, and MyNewsLA court-record reporting.

The corroboration supplement is `starintel-documents-part-007.jsonl`; it supersedes the earlier publication-gate-pending annotations for those three subjects.

## Evidence boundary

This packet is an intake and corroboration layer. It does not convert accusations into findings or charges into convictions. Later court dispositions belong in later records with their own sources.
