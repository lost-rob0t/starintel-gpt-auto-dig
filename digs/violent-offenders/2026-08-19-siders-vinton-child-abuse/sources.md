## Protected-victim handling

Public availability is not sufficient reason to ingest victim-sensitive information.

The packet must not store or reproduce:

- names, initials, aliases, or identity-resolving victim records;
- exact addresses associated with protected minors;
- dates of birth or exact ages;
- schools, homeschool records, medical providers, foster/placement details, phone/email/social accounts, photographs, or unique physical/developmental descriptors;
- genealogy, household role, or kinship details that could narrow a protected victim's identity.

Anonymous research labels such as `Minor Victim A` are non-identity-bearing handles only. They must never be resolved to a real person or used as a bridge to outside identifying data.

Aggregate victim counts may be retained only where necessary to accurately represent an official charge structure or procedural statement.
