## Adult defendants

- Elizabeth Siders
- Gary Siders Jr.
- Gary Siders Sr.
- Christina Siders

The Ohio Attorney General reported that all four were arraigned July 1 in Vinton County Court of Common Pleas and that each was charged with 16 counts of endangering children, a second-degree felony.

## June 30 rescue

Ohio authorities said investigators executing court-authorized search warrants in Hamden located 16 children in the residence. The children were removed, medically evaluated, and placed in temporary state custody. Some required elevated medical care and were reported in serious condition.

Investigators characterized the case as involving prolonged and extreme interfamilial neglect and abuse. Associated Press reporting independently described allegations of prolonged confinement, severe sanitation problems, lack of schooling, medical neglect, and major developmental impairment among some of the children.

## Current procedural posture

Current reporting says the four defendants have pleaded not guilty.

On August 18, The U.S. Sun reported that Vinton County Prosecutor William Archer opposed Elizabeth Siders' request for release and said a grand jury was considering additional “serious felony charges” against her. The report did not identify those potential charges.

That item is recorded as an **attributed procedural report**, not as a filed indictment or adjudicated charge. The Ohio Attorney General had already said on July 1 that additional charges were expected as the investigation continued.
