## August 21 indictment update

A Vinton County grand jury issued indictments in three linked criminal investigations. The August 21 release originated with the Ohio Attorney General's Office and was republished by Richland Source.

### Investigation One

The investigation opened in December 2025 and concerns alleged sexual abuse of protected minors.

- Josh Saunders: three counts of rape (F1) and one count of gross sexual imposition (F3).
- Brandon Henderson Sr.: one count of endangering children (F2), two counts of endangering children (F3), four counts of gross sexual imposition (F3), four counts of gross sexual imposition (F4), eight counts of sexual battery (F4), and two counts of endangering children (M1).

The protected minors in this investigation are not identified or profiled.

### Investigation Two

Information from Investigation One prompted a second BCI investigation in March 2026. The August 21 indictments charge:

- Gary Siders Jr.: two counts of sexual battery (F3) and two counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (F3).
- Elizabeth Siders: two counts of sexual battery (F3) and two counts of unlawful sexual conduct with a minor (F3).

For this packet, the protected minor connected to these allegations is referred to only as **Minor Victim A**. `Minor Victim A` is an anonymous, non-identity-bearing research label. The packet does not attempt to resolve, infer, or publish that person's identity, family relationship, age, school, medical history, placement, image, social accounts, or other identifying attributes.

### Investigation Three

The June 30 search led to the child-endangerment investigation already covered by this packet. The new indictments supersede the earlier preliminary count summary for current procedural reporting:

- Gary Siders Jr.: 19 counts total — two F2 endangering-children counts, 10 F3 counts, and seven M1 counts.
- Elizabeth Siders: 19 counts total — two F2 endangering-children counts, 10 F3 counts, and seven M1 counts.
- Gary Siders Sr.: 16 counts total — 10 F3 endangering-children counts and six M1 counts.
- Christina Siders: 19 counts total — two F2 endangering-children counts, 10 F3 counts, and seven M1 counts.

The prosecution says some counts are charged in the alternative. The packet therefore preserves the filed count structure and does not infer a final number of distinct criminal acts from the raw count total.

## June 30 discovery and prior posture

Ohio authorities previously reported that investigators executing court-authorized search warrants in Hamden located 16 children in the residence. The children were removed and placed in temporary state custody. The four Siders adults were initially arraigned July 1 on child-endangering charges.

The previous August 18 packet state recorded a report that additional serious felony charges were under grand-jury consideration. The August 21 indictments resolve that procedural uncertainty. The historical August 18 report remains provenance, but it is no longer the current charge posture.

## Victim privacy contract

Hard rules:

- no exact residential address associated with protected victims;

Aggregate victim counts may be retained only when they are necessary to understand a public charging instrument or official procedural statement.
