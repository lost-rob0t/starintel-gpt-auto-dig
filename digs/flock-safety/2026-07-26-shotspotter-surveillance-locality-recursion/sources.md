## Source treatment

- Government legislation, minutes, audits, court decisions, and official policy pages establish official actions and recorded findings.
- Independent evaluations establish their own study results, not universal performance.
- SoundThinking and Flock pages establish vendor-described capabilities and controls, not locality configuration.
- The Policing Project privacy audit is used with its disclosed vendor-funding and governance conflict attached.
- Cross-locality metrics are not treated as directly interchangeable without accounting for coverage, definitions, dispatch policy, and study period.
- Precise sensor locations and live operational details are intentionally excluded.
