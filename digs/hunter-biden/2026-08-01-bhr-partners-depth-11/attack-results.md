# Depth-11 attack results

## Control event

On October 19, 2025:

- Nijjar Holdings ceased as Indul's PSC;
- Andrew Jonathan Sherick and Vinesh Harakhchand Shah left the board;
- Stewart Carson, Michael Ip and Natalie Sarich-Dayton joined the board;
- Indul began reporting that it knew of no registrable PSC.

Brownes announced the acquisition on October 23, 2025. The transaction included Indul's Hungerford production facility, and the stated rationale was expanding Hunt and Brew ready-to-drink coffee production.

## Receivership context

The acquisition occurred while McGrathNicol was managing the sale of Brownes through the Australian Zhiran share receivership. Public sources do not identify:

- the exact Brownes/DairyWest acquisition entity;
- the consideration or funding source;
- receiver or creditor consent;
- the post-acquisition registered shareholder;
- the legal reason no registrable PSC was reported.
