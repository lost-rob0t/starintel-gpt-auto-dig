# Source ledger — BHR Partners depth 2

## 1. Hunter Biden deposition transcript

Existing source ID: `starintel:source:house-hunter-biden-deposition-2024-02-28`

Primary extracts used:

- Rosemont Seneca Thornton bank records showed January 23 and January 29, 2014 wires of $167,000 and $247,800 to BHR-related beneficiaries, totaling $414,800.
- Hunter Biden said the account was Devon Archer's, not his, and that he lacked control or authority over it.
- A Morgan Stanley document shown as exhibit 5 listed Rosemont Seneca Partners with an `IBO` notation for Rosemont Seneca Thornton; a separate account agreement listed Devon Archer as the sole signatory.
- Committee counsel described a December 2014 Rosemont Seneca Bohai purchase of another 10 percent BHR block. Hunter Biden said he was not directly aware of it and denied control of the account.
- Hunter Biden described the 2019 $250,000 Jonathan Li wire as a loan against BHR equity.
- He testified that Skaneateles held the equity and that he sold Skaneateles to Kevin Morris.

## 2. Kevin Morris interview transcript

Source ID: `starintel:source:house-kevin-morris-interview-2024-01-18`

Primary extracts used:

- Morris said he acquired Skaneateles and understood it to own the BHR piece.
- He agreed that a 10 percent stake sounded right and said he currently owned that interest through one of his corporate entities.
- He recalled purchasing Skaneateles in 2021.
- His live recollection of the purchase price and loan structure was uncertain.
- He said there was a written agreement, no third-party valuation, no BHR payouts since acquisition, and no current valuation as of the interview.

## 3. House impeachment inquiry report

Source ID: `starintel:source:house-impeachment-inquiry-report-2024-08-19`

The report cites a January 25, 2024 letter from Morris's counsel stating:

- acquisition date: November 17, 2021;
- written agreement negotiated by separate counsel;
- consideration: assumption of $157,729.69 in remaining debt;
- no Hunter Biden right to repurchase Skaneateles membership interests.

These are counsel representations reproduced or summarized in a congressional majority report, not an independently adjudicated valuation.

## 4. Federal tax indictment

Source ID: `starintel:source:doj-hunter-biden-tax-indictment-2023-12-07`

The indictment alleges that Skaneateles was 75 percent owned by Hunter Biden and 25 percent by an unnamed business associate when it transferred approximately $666,572 to Hunter Biden in September 2017.

Qualification: the source does not state that this transfer was BHR-related. The record is included only to preserve the broader ownership context of Skaneateles.
