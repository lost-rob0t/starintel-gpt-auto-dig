## Extracts used

### Skaneateles formation and account visibility

Schwerin testified that he and Hunter Biden created Skaneateles before Seneca Global Advisors to hold their business interests. He also said he had access to Skaneateles accounts.

He separately testified that Devon Archer owned and controlled Rosemont Seneca Bohai, controlled the Rosemont Seneca Thornton account, and that Schwerin lacked contemporaneous visibility into those accounts.

### Joint BHR interest

Schwerin testified that he and Hunter Biden obtained a joint 10 percent BHR interest through Skaneateles around 2017.

He specified that the interest was 10 percent of BHR's management company.

### Skaneateles ownership

Schwerin testified that Hunter Biden owned 75 percent of Skaneateles and Schwerin owned 25 percent.

This upgrades the same ownership split previously preserved only as a source-qualified allegation from the federal tax indictment.

### Business unwind

Schwerin testified that the partners began unwinding their businesses in September 2017 and completed the legal work in March 2019.

He described a no-cash swap:

- Schwerin received the Rosemont Seneca Partners investments business.
- Hunter Biden received the BHR and Rosemont Seneca Advisors side.

### Valuation

Schwerin recalled that BHR's registered-capital valuation placed the joint 10 percent management-company interest at approximately $450,000.

The figure is treated as qualified witness recollection and still requires reconciliation with the approximately $420,000 original-investment figure discussed in later testimony and counsel correspondence.

### Board participation

Schwerin testified that he attended BHR board meetings in China, normally when Hunter Biden could not attend, and that they never attended a BHR board meeting together.

This does not by itself establish Schwerin's formal legal status as a director or alternate.
