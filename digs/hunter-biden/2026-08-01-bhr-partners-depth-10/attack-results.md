# Depth-10 attack results

## Governance chronology recovered

The February 2026 exchange response provides five board-control periods:

1. **May 2018–February 2021**
   - Australian Zhiran: Cao Rui, Chen Junfeng, Ye Xiu / Dong Xiulan, Ren Song and Anthony Magdi Girgis.
   - Brownes: Ye Xiu / Dong Xiulan, Ren Song and Anthony Magdi Girgis.

2. **February–June 2021**
   - Both boards: Cui Mindong, Ren Song and Anthony Magdi Girgis.

3. **June 2021–November 2024**
   - Both boards: Cui Mindong, Cui Xintong and Natalie Sarich-Dayton.

4. **November 2024–April 2025**
   - Both boards: Cui Mindong, Cui Xintong and Chen Ting.

5. **After the April 2025 takeover**
   - Both boards: Stewart Carson, Michael Ip and Natalie Sarich-Dayton.

## Fund control recovered

The response states that Chai Xiu and related parties controlled Shanghai Xiangmin and its overseas underlying assets from June 29, 2020 until the April 2025 takeover. It also records the original BHR Fund VIII investment committee and the general unanimous-vote rule.

## Security chain recovered

- December 24, 2020: Changchun Lianxin pledged 90 percent of Jilin Zhiran.
- December 25, 2020: the fund pledged 99.99 percent of Changchun Lianxin.
- December 26, 2020: the Jilin Zhiran / Australian group executed the group guarantee and general security deed.
- December 22, 2023: the debt matured unpaid.
- April 2025: domestic arbitration and overseas takeover notices were issued.
- December 25, 2025: the Beijing Arbitration Commission issued its award.
- February 14, 2026: the award had not yet been executed and no underlying-equity auction was reported.
