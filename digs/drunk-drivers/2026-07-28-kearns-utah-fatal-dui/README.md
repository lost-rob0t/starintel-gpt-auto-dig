Initial incident packet for the `drunk-drivers` dataset.

## Driver

**Charmayne Huerta Guzman** — also reported as Charmayne Huerta-Guzman and Charmayne Guzman.

## Incident

On Jan. 14, 2024, Guzman drove a Kia Sorento near 4800 West and 5200 South in Kearns, Utah. Public court reporting states that she was traveling an estimated 77 mph in a 35 mph zone on a wet road, crossed into oncoming traffic, and struck a Volkswagen Jetta head-on. Her measured blood-alcohol level was reported as 0.241%.

Five-year-old Lennyn Lilic died from her injuries. Her father, Semir Lilic, and other occupants were injured.

Guzman later pleaded guilty to murder and three negligent-operation injury charges. On Jan. 5, 2026, she received consecutive prison terms.
