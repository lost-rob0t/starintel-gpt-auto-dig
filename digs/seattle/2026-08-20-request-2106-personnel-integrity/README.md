# Seattle request #2106 — command and integrity timeline, depth 1

Stable request key: `seattle-police-integrity-motive-2105`

## Findings

- **Shon Barnes was SPD chief on June 17, 2026**, when Mayor Katie Wilson announced expanded police personnel and enforcement at 12th & Jackson and North Beacon Hill.
- **Andre Sayles became interim chief on July 30, 2026.** Do not back-project his later title onto the June operation.
- SPD's current biography says **Robert Brown moved from West Precinct command to Patrol Operations in August 2025 and became Assistant Chief in May 2026**. A stale resource page still labels him West Precinct commander; the dated biography is the stronger temporal evidence for June 2026.
- **Heidi Tuttle** remains the documented South Precinct captain. Chinatown/International District is West Precinct geography; Beacon Hill is South Precinct geography.
- SPD documented a **March 20 West Patrol proactive emphasis at 12th & Jackson** targeting narcotics use and illegal vending. Enforcement there therefore predates the June 17 mayoral announcement.
- Seattle's March surveillance-pilot material places part of Chinatown-ID through 12th & Jackson within the Real Time Crime Center coverage area and describes RTCC operators as civilian SPD employees. That is a support/control layer, not evidence that a particular operator directed an enforcement action.

## Integrity posture

The reviewed primary records establish command, geography, and dated policy/enforcement activity. They do **not** establish corruption, selective enforcement, improper influence, or political motive by Barnes, Sayles, Brown, Tuttle, or another named employee.

Absence of a public OIG report title is not proof that no complaint exists. It only means no reviewed public OIG item was enough to support that conclusion in this pass.
