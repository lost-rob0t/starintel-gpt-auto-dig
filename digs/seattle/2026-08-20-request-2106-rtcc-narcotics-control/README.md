# Seattle request #2106 — RTCC/narcotics control chain, depth 2

Stable request key: `seattle-police-integrity-motive-2105`

Parent target: `starintel:investigation-target:seattle-2106-operation-orders-command-chain-depth-1`

## Findings

- SPD incident `2026-25701` documents an **active narcotics surveillance operation at 12th Ave S / S Jackson St on January 27, 2026**. Officers asked an RTCC analyst to locate a known felony-warrant suspect; RTCC staff found him and sent his picture to arrest teams as they moved in.
- This is stronger than general RTCC coverage evidence: it directly joins **RTCC analyst support → narcotics surveillance → arrest teams → 12th & Jackson** before the June 17 mayoral enforcement expansion.
- Seattle Executive Order 2024-02 formally established the Downtown Activation Team as a cross-department place-based control layer including SPD, with operations in Downtown, Chinatown-ID, and Little Saigon. The mayoral release explicitly describes targeted enforcement as part of DAT operations.
- Seattle's police-accountability policy-recommendation index has active department-level controls concerning **Employee Timekeeping and Overtime Tracking** (`2025OPA-0152 / 2025COMP-0016`) and **On-call premium** (`2026OPA-0135 / 2026COMP-0002`). These are relevant control surfaces, but they are not evidence that the January or June 12th & Jackson operations violated those controls.
- The Council budget record separately identifies a 2026 SPD overtime increase and quarterly staffing/overtime/performance reporting requirements. Those records create a concrete payroll/accountability join for operation-specific staffing, but do not establish which appropriation funded a particular deployment.

## Evidence state

`verified_fact`:

- narcotics surveillance was underway at 12th & Jackson in incident `2026-25701`;
- an RTCC analyst assisted the operation by locating the suspect and sending a picture to arrest teams;
- DAT is a formal multi-department coordination structure that includes SPD and targeted enforcement;
- department-level overtime/timekeeping controls and quarterly reporting requirements exist.

`unresolved`:

- the identity and supervisor of the RTCC analyst in incident `2026-25701`;
- the operation-order author and approving supervisor;
- the exact unit/watch/K3/CRG chain for that operation;
- the RTCC access/audit entries associated with the incident;
- matching staffing, overtime, on-call, or payroll approval records;
- whether the later March-June K3/DAT operations used the same command/tasking path.

No reviewed primary record in this slice establishes corruption, selective enforcement, improper political influence, or political motive by a named SPD employee.
