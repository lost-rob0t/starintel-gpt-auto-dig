Research date: 2026-08-12

I. FORMAL ORGANIZATIONAL RELATIONSHIP

McKinsey & Company is publicly listed by WEF as a STRATEGIC PARTNER. [A/HIGH]
WEF describes Strategic Partners as a community of roughly 100 leading companies working closely with WEF to help shape industry, regional, and global agendas.
WEF’s broader partner page states that partners are a driving force behind Forum programs and engage in WEF Centres to shape the future and access expert networks for strategic decision-making.

This is stronger than mere Davos attendance: it is a formal organization-to-organization partnership category maintained by WEF.

III. JOINT WEF–MCKINSEY PROGRAMS / PRODUCTS

Global Lighthouse Network
- WEF says the Global Lighthouse Network was co-founded with McKinsey & Company and is counselled by an advisory board. [A/HIGH]
- It is a long-running advanced-manufacturing/technology network.

Resilience Consortium
- WEF and McKinsey launched/advanced “Resilience for Sustainable Inclusive Growth.”
- WEF describes the Resilience Consortium as a public-private leadership effort involving government ministers, CEOs, and heads of international organizations.
- Bob Sternfels and WEF leadership repeatedly front associated work. [A/HIGH]

Global Cooperation Barometer
- WEF’s 2024, 2025, and 2026 Global Cooperation Barometer work is developed in collaboration with McKinsey.
- The 2026 edition lists WEF President/CEO Børge Brende and McKinsey Global Managing Partner Bob Sternfels in the preface/media briefing. [A/HIGH]

Additional co-produced or partnership work surfaced on the WEF McKinsey organization page includes:
- Securing Minerals for the Energy Transition
- public-private-philanthropic climate/nature partnership work
- women’s health / McKinsey Health Institute work
- nature and natural-capital work
- advanced manufacturing and Global Lighthouse reports
- resilience / emerging-market work
- workforce / future-of-work work.

VII. WHAT THE WEF “MEMBERSHIP LIST” EVIDENCE DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW

Strong/explicit:
- McKinsey is a WEF Strategic Partner.
- WEF has an organization page for McKinsey.
- WEF publicly associates numerous named McKinsey contributors with the organization.
- Multiple McKinsey staff are/were YGLs.
- Multiple current McKinsey board members have WEF contributor/person/council footprints.
- WEF and McKinsey co-found/co-produce programs and research.

Not established merely by the above:
- that every listed contributor is a formal WEF member;
- that every McKinsey board member belongs to WEF;
- that WEF directs McKinsey or McKinsey directs WEF;
- that joint research automatically becomes government policy.
