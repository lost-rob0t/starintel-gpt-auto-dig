GOVERNMENT OF CANADA ↔ WORLD ECONOMIC FORUM LINKS
Research date: 2026-08-12

I. CANADA’S OWN DESCRIPTION OF WEF ENGAGEMENT

Global Affairs Canada briefing materials say the Forum provides a multi-layered platform to advance Canadian priorities and shape the Forum’s agenda in a “unique ecosystem” bringing together political, business, academic, and civil-society actors.

Canadian engagement described by Global Affairs includes:
- funding specific WEF initiatives;
- participation in WEF-related bodies;
- collaboration on reports and initiatives;
- ministerial/senior-official participation at Davos and regional meetings;
- bilateral/pull-aside networking;
- engagement on trade/investment, development finance, food security, humanitarian assistance, resilience, Arctic issues, and other areas.

The same materials describe WEF platforms/initiatives as agenda-setting mechanisms that mobilize leadership, expertise, and resources from government, business, civil society, and international organizations. [A/HIGH]

II. KNOWN TRAVELLER DIGITAL IDENTITY (KTDI)

Transport Canada announced in January 2018 that Canada would collaborate with WEF and partners on a Known Traveller Digital Identity prototype.
Technical concepts included:
- advanced biometrics;
- cryptography;
- distributed-ledger technology;
- mobile sharing of identity/information;
- interaction among travelers, governments, and travel providers.

Canada and the Netherlands were to design a proof-of-concept pilot.
The underlying report was developed/consulted with actors including:
- Accenture
- Government of Canada
- Google
- Visa
- INTERPOL
- ICAO
- U.S. Department of Homeland Security
- U.S. Department of Commerce
- IATA
- Marriott
- Hilton
- World Travel & Tourism Council
- others.

This is a direct public-private policy/technology collaboration edge. [A/HIGH]

III. CANADIAN FUNDING TO WEF

Open Government / Global Affairs records surfaced WEF grants/contributions including:
- $1,000,000 — Dec 23, 2014
- $6,000,000 — Sep 29, 2015
- $10,000,000 — Dec 14, 2015
- $5,933,063 — Mar 16, 2020, Global Plastic Action Partnership
- $1,500,000 — Feb 21, 2025 to Apr 30, 2028, Global Alliance for Trade Facilitation Phase 2

Prior research also surfaced later/related biodiversity/plastics commitments; each should be re-fetched as individual grant records before final StarIntel publication.

Important: funding a WEF-run international development/trade/environment program is not the same as paying WEF to set Canadian domestic policy. It is, however, a documented government-to-WEF financial edge. [A/HIGH]

IV. HISTORICAL CANADIAN WEF TRUSTEES

WEF’s February 2021 Board of Trustees roster listed:
- Mark Carney
- Chrystia Freeland, then Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance of Canada

WEF’s March 2022 Board of Trustees roster again listed both Carney and Freeland.
This is formal WEF governance participation, not merely event attendance. [A/HIGH]

V. MCKINSEY / CANADIAN GOVERNMENT / WEF TRIANGLE

Documented structural overlap includes:
- McKinsey = WEF Strategic Partner.
- Andrew Pickersgill = current McKinsey board chair + WEF Agenda Contributor + former McKinsey Canada managing partner + federal ACEG support lead + historical Century Initiative founder/board director.
- Dominic Barton = former McKinsey Global Managing Partner + multiple WEF roles/events + federal ACEG chair + Century Initiative founder/board.
- WEF programs involve public-private agenda-setting/implementation work; Canada explicitly participates in this ecosystem.

This triangulation supports a network-influence research hypothesis, but not a centralized-command conclusion.
