I. PRC FOREIGN INTERFERENCE — OFFICIAL CANADIAN BASELINE

Canadian official/intelligence/inquiry records describe the People’s Republic of China as the most active foreign-interference actor in Canada. [A/HIGH]

Documented threat descriptions include:
- targeting all levels of government;
- targeting civil society, communities, media entities, and academia;
- influencing political decision-making;
- cultivating relationships over time;
- exploiting or influencing political processes;
- leveraging community organizations and proxies;
- disinformation/information operations;
- cyber/espionage activity;
- efforts tied to trade, business, research advantages, and regime legitimacy.

Foreign Interference Commission / CSIS material emphasizes that PRC activity is pragmatic and may support whichever party or person is perceived as friendliest to PRC interests rather than being rigidly partisan. [A/HIGH]

II. WHAT THE OFFICIAL RECORD DOES NOT SUPPORT

- It does not establish that the PRC controls the Canadian government.
- The inquiry upheld the overall integrity of recent federal-election outcomes while documenting significant interference attempts.
- Intelligence reporting contains uncertainty and must not automatically be treated as proven criminal fact.
