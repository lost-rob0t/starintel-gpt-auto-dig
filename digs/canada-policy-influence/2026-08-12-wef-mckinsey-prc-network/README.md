# Canada policy-influence network — McKinsey, WEF, immigration, and PRC interference

## Scope

This public-source Auto-Dig packet records the evidence gathered on four analytically separate tracks:

1. McKinsey & Company relationships with the Government of Canada and federal administrative transformation.
2. McKinsey ↔ World Economic Forum institutional/personnel links.
3. Canadian immigration-policy networks around the Advisory Council on Economic Growth, Century Initiative, and IRCC.
4. Documented PRC/CCP foreign-interference activity in Canada.

The packet deliberately distinguishes **formal authority**, **consulting/advisory work**, **agenda-setting**, **membership/governance**, **event participation**, **funding/contracting**, **professional-network overlap**, and **covert foreign interference**. A relationship edge is not treated as proof of command, corruption, conspiracy, or policy causation.

## High-confidence findings

- McKinsey is listed by the World Economic Forum as a **Strategic Partner**. WEF says Strategic Partners work closely with the Forum on industry, regional, and global agendas.
- McKinsey's current Shareholders Council is its board of directors. Explicit WEF footprints were surfaced for multiple current members, including Andrew Pickersgill, Gassan Al-Kibsi, Fabian Billing, Liz Hilton Segel, Acha Leke, Bob Sternfels, and Kurt Strovink. The exact WEF relationship differs by person and is not collapsed into generic “membership.”
- McKinsey and WEF have co-developed recurring programs/research including the Global Lighthouse Network, resilience work, and the Global Cooperation Barometer.
- Government of Canada briefing material describes Canada as participating in WEF bodies, collaborating on reports and initiatives, funding specific initiatives, attending Forum meetings, and using that ecosystem to shape agenda discussions.
- Canada and WEF directly collaborated on the **Known Traveller Digital Identity** prototype.
- Historical Century Initiative material identifies **Mark Wiseman, Dominic Barton, Willa Black, Goldy Hyder, and Andrew Pickersgill** as founders/board directors.
- Century Initiative publicly presented a **CI–McKinsey “Achieving Better Integration Outcomes”** report; a McKinsey career profile also records a former Toronto analyst leaving the firm to start Century Initiative and later returning.
- Dominic Barton chaired the federal **Advisory Council on Economic Growth (ACEG)** while McKinsey supplied a pro-bono research/secretariat team. Witnesses said the Council, not McKinsey staff, made the recommendations.
- ACEG recommended raising permanent immigration toward **450,000 annually over five years**, plus specialized-talent, international-student, and accreditation reforms. ACEG's own later report described interactive work with Finance Canada/departments and identified government implementation of several associated recommendations.
- IRCC states that McKinsey held roughly **$27.7 million** in transformation contracts covering diagnostics, transformation strategy, roadmap, operating models, digital-service work, processing improvements, and digital-talent recruitment. IRCC created a Chief Transformation Officer function and transformation teams after considering recommendations.
- The Auditor General found **97 McKinsey contracts worth $209 million** across the audited federal/Crown-corporation population from 2011–2023, with about $200 million spent and about 70% of contracts noncompetitive; the audit identified procurement-policy failures.
- McKinsey also appears in the early Canada Infrastructure Bank network through ACEG research support and later CIB consulting on investment criteria, governance/risk systems, and related work. Parliamentary witnesses disputed stronger causal/conflict interpretations.
- Canadian official assessments and the Foreign Interference Commission describe the **PRC as Canada's most active foreign-interference actor**, using state-directed and proxy mechanisms to target government and civil society. This is kept separate from ordinary China-related professional/business ties.
- The same official record does **not** establish that the PRC controls Canada's government or that federal election outcomes were determined by foreign interference.

## Evidence discipline

- A WEF profile, Agenda Contributor page, YGL designation, trustee role, council role, Strategic Partner listing, or event appearance is recorded as the specific documented relationship only.
- McKinsey consulting/research support is not treated as proof that McKinsey exercised sovereign governmental authority.
- Similarity between an advisory recommendation and later government policy is not alone proof of causation.
- PRC/China business, academic, or professional ties are not treated as CCP direction or foreign interference without additional evidence.
- Search non-hits are treated as unresolved coverage gaps, never proof of absence.
