## Objective

Retrieve and reconcile Michael Kratsios's signed ethics agreement, Certification of Ethics Agreement Compliance, divestment-completion dates, recusals, authorizations and waivers involving Scale AI, Thiel-linked entities and former affiliations.

## What is established

The Senate nomination record is explicit about the **commitments** Kratsios made before confirmation:

- his Thiel Capital 401(k) would continue without further employer contributions;
- his Scale AI 401(k) and HSA would continue without further employer contributions;
- he held Scale AI equity/options, bitcoin and ethereum;
- if confirmed, he said he would divest those assets pursuant to his ethics agreement;
- he said he would follow the ethics agreement's recusal obligations;
- he identified former Stanford, Foundation for American Innovation and University of Oklahoma roles as additional recusal subjects.

The Senate confirmed Kratsios on **March 25, 2025**.

OGE explains that, unless an ethics agreement specifies otherwise, PAS appointees agree to complete required actions within **90 days of confirmation** and submit a **Certification of Ethics Agreement Compliance** within the same 90-day period. That makes June 23, 2025 the ordinary 90-day point for this appointment, absent a different deadline or extension in the actual agreement.

## Public disclosure snapshot

ProPublica's current Trump-team disclosure page, derived from government ethics records, exposes the nominee financial disclosure and later transaction data. The page shows:

- Scale AI vested stock options valued at $1M–$5M;
- Scale AI Class B common shares valued at $1M–$5M;
- bitcoin valued at $100K–$250K and ethereum at $15K–$50K in the displayed disclosure;
- a Thiel Capital 401(k), Scale AI 401(k) and Scale AI HSA;
- many later public-market transactions during 2025–2026.

The visible transaction table does **not** display a Scale AI, bitcoin or ethereum sale. That absence is not proof that required divestment did not occur. The compliance certification, private-company disposition records, amendments or other ethics documents may supply the missing completion evidence.

There is also a date discrepancy worth preserving rather than silently normalizing: the official Senate questionnaire says Kratsios worked at Scale AI from March 2021 through **January 17, 2024**, while ProPublica's parsed employment-agreement text displays an apparent January 17, **2021** end date even though the same disclosure page separately lists him as Scale AI Managing Director from March 2021 through January 2025. The Senate record is therefore used for the nominee's stated employment end date while the conflicting parsed disclosure value remains documented as an unresolved source discrepancy.
