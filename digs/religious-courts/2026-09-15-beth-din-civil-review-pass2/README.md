## Why these cases

Current-main and PR dedupe found no existing records for `Matter of Schwimmer v Malinas`, **Beth Din Bais Hora'ah Etz Chaim**, or `Berg v Berg` (2011). Both opinions are official New York court records and materially improve the requested civil-review/vacatur ledger because they show the difference between:

Routine judicial correction is not being relabeled as corruption.

## Matter of Schwimmer v Malinas — 2013 NY Slip Op 50158(U)

The Kings County Supreme Court opinion identifies the tribunal as **Beth Din Bais Hora'ah Etz Chaim** and names the arbitration award dated March 7, 2012. The respondent sought vacatur on several theories, including alleged evidentiary unfairness, inability to copy a computer hard drive, limits on cross-examination, differing Hebrew/English arbitration language, manifest disregard of law, and irrationality.

The court rejected the claimed misconduct/vacatur grounds. It held, among other things, that the respondent had not established prejudicial arbitral misconduct, that the procedural cross-examination objection had been waived and was also without merit, and that the award had a rational basis.

The court nevertheless refused to confirm one attorney-fee provision. Because the arbitration agreement did not authorize attorneys' fees, the court held that the arbitrators exceeded their authority on that limited remedy. The cross-petition to vacate the award was denied and confirmation was granted except for that legal-expense provision.

**Evidence state:** misconduct allegations rejected / award substantially confirmed / limited remedy modified for excess of authority. This is counterevidence to a misconduct claim, not proof that every tribunal procedure was ideal.

The same opinion publicly identifies Rabbi Chaim Kohn as dean/chief rabbi of the tribunal and reproduces an arbitration agreement naming Chaim Kohn, Moshe Yeshaya Bochner, and Bentzion Meisels as arbitrators. This pass preserves the institutional identity but does not create broader person profiles or infer conduct outside the case record.

## Berg v Berg — 2011 NY Slip Op 05440

The Appellate Division, Second Department reviewed a Beth Din award in a matrimonial arbitration. The opinion does **not** identify a stable tribunal name, so this pass does not invent or merge an institution identity.

The appellant alleged impropriety/misconduct and argued that one party-appointed rabbi was not neutral. The appellate court held that he failed to prove prejudicial misconduct and explained that, under the tripartite structure the parties had chosen, a party-selected arbitrator was not individually expected to be neutral in the same way as the presiding neutral arbitrator.

The court also rejected the remaining general vacatur arguments. It did, however, vacate the portion awarding the other party an attorney's fee and all future legal expenses because the arbitration agreement did not expressly provide for attorneys' fees.

**Evidence state:** alleged misconduct/partiality not established / award otherwise upheld / attorney-fee remedy vacated because it exceeded contractual authority. Again, judicial modification is not classified as corruption.
