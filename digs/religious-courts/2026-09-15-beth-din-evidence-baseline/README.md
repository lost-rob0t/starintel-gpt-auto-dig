## Scope and identity discipline

This pass establishes three deliberately separate records:

1. **October 2, 2013 Wolmark/Epstein ad-hoc beth din** — a specific proceeding convened in Martin Wolmark's Monsey office during the federal get-coercion conspiracy. The DOJ Information says Wolmark, Mendel Epstein, and Jay Goldstein convened the proceeding to issue a psak din authorizing coercion. Wolmark later pleaded guilty to conspiracy to travel interstate to commit extortion and was sentenced to 38 months; Epstein and Binyamin Stimler were convicted at trial in the related kidnapping conspiracy. This packet does **not** identify that ad-hoc proceeding as Beth Din of America or as any other standing tribunal.
2. **Beth Din of America** — a current New York arbitration organization resolved from its first-party rules page and the 2026 `Matter of Redacted v Redacted` opinion. The husband alleged misconduct in challenging a 2023 award, but the proceedings settled and were discontinued in October 2025. The allegation is therefore retained as **alleged / not adjudicated**, not promoted to a finding.
3. **The Beth Din of the United States of America, Inc.** — the exact corporate name in the 2026 `Mittel` appellate caption. The First Department affirmed dismissal because the arbitration agreement incorporated rules conferring arbitral immunity. This is a procedural/immunity disposition, not a merits finding that misconduct did or did not occur. It remains a separate organization record pending evidence resolving any relationship to other named batei din.

- DOJ records state the October 2, 2013 beth din was convened in Wolmark's office by Wolmark, Mendel Epstein, and Jay Goldstein and was used in the planned forced-get operation.

### Alleged / not adjudicated

- `Matter of Redacted v Redacted`, 2026 NY Slip Op 50787(U), records the husband's allegation that Beth Din of America acted irrationally and refused to hear evidence surrounding a postnuptial agreement. The confirmation/vacatur proceedings settled and were discontinued, so the opinion does not decide that allegation.

### Procedural / counterevidence

- `Mittel v Beth Din of the United States of America, Inc.`, 2026 NY Slip Op 03076, unanimously affirmed dismissal on arbitral-immunity grounds derived from the agreed rules. The packet does not transform the existence of litigation into evidence of corruption.
- Beth Din of America currently publishes rules/procedures for its arbitration proceedings. Those rules are preserved as a governance baseline for later case-by-case comparison; publication of rules is neither proof of compliance nor proof of violation.
