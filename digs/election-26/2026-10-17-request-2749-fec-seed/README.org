* Scope and evidence
This is a *bounded research packet* for operator request #2749 (request key =auto-dig:election-26-fec-2026=). The source is the U.S. Federal Election Commission's own public 2026-cycle committee pages, inspected on 2026-10-17. All figures are the FEC's published *aggregates*, covering 2025-01-01 through 2026-08-31. The FEC notes that newly filed summary data may take up to 48 hours to appear.

- DNC SERVICES CORP / DEMOCRATIC NATIONAL COMMITTEE, FEC committee =C00010603=: [[https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00010603/?cycle=2026][official FEC source]]. Total receipts USD 228,122,454.22; itemized individual contributions USD 69,003,510.56; total disbursements USD 233,343,989.19.
- REPUBLICAN NATIONAL COMMITTEE, FEC committee =C00003418=: [[https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00003418/?cycle=2026][official FEC source]]. Total receipts USD 316,253,037.57; itemized individual contributions USD 101,243,230.32; total disbursements USD 228,749,585.28.

Both committees are included under identical source and parsing rules. These amounts are neither netted nor disaggregated into individual FEC itemizations, and no conclusions about specific donors, transaction dates, amendments, refunds, or underlying sources of funds are drawn. These are *not* contribution records or evidence of any connection between specific people. Do not add these aggregates to itemized transactions or interpret them as an independent receipt ledger.
