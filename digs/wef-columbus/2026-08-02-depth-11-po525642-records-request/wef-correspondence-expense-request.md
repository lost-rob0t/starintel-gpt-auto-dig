## Records requested

1. Emails, attachments, calendar invitations, meeting notes, memoranda, and responsive electronic-message exports involving the listed people, entities, or keywords.

2. Event invitations, registrations, accreditation, participant agreements, profile-authorization records, speaker agreements, task-force records, agendas, and submitted materials.

3. Travel authorizations, airfare, lodging, ground transportation, per diem, reimbursement, purchasing-card, and expense-report records.

4. Dues, sponsorship, registration-fee, subscription, invoice, payment, donation, grant, and funding-source records involving the World Economic Forum or listed programs.

5. Records allocating City funds, Smart Columbus contract funds, or `PO525642` resources to a WEF-related event, task force, profile, deliverable, meeting, or expense.

6. Records identifying the funding source for any responsive travel, fee, sponsorship, or participation expense, including whether the expense was paid by the City, Columbus Partnership, Smart Columbus, WEF, another organization, or the participant personally.

## Scope boundary

This request does not seek unrelated communications, personnel files, or personal financial information. The production may be limited to responsive keyword hits and the listed custodians. Banking details, taxpayer-identification numbers, signatures, home addresses, and unrelated personal information may be redacted.

Please provide native electronic files when available and preserve filenames, timestamps, sender/recipient fields, attachment relationships, and other readily available metadata.
