# Draft public-records request: 2026 Citywide Memberships reconciliation

To the City of Columbus public-records custodian:

Please provide the following public records for **December 1, 2025 through August 2, 2026**, concerning:

- Ordinance `0223-2026`
- Authorization/ACPO `ACPO012949`
- Main account `63975`
- The 2026 Annual Citywide Memberships authorization
- Vendor account `001891`
- Purchase orders `PO546851` and `PO559530`
- Invoices `20787` and `20801`
- Invoice vouchers `APIN111443947` and `APIN111475818`

## Records requested

1. Authorization and accounting-distribution records showing the complete accounting string, including main account, department, division, fund, subfund, program, object class, optional fields, source-document header, and source-document line.

2. All purchase orders, purchase-order lines, requisitions, amendments, encumbrance documents, and release documents charged to ordinance `0223-2026`, `ACPO012949`, or main account `63975`.

3. All invoice images, invoice-register entries, voucher-application details, payment journals, checks, electronic-payment records, remittance records, cancellations, credits, refunds, reversals, and settlement records associated with the identifiers above.

4. Authorization-level reports showing the original $150,000 ceiling, total encumbrances, total expenditures, released encumbrances, remaining balance, and closeout status.

5. Vendor-master records sufficient to reconcile each vendor account to its legal and payee names.

6. Records showing how invoices `20787` and `20801` were settled, including whether either invoice was duplicated, corrected, credited, cancelled, combined into another settlement, or remained outstanding.

## Preferred format

Please provide ledger-style records in CSV or XLSX format and document images in searchable PDF or their native electronic format. Please preserve native record identifiers and column headers.

Bank-account numbers, routing numbers, taxpayer-identification numbers, signatures, home addresses, and unrelated personal information may be redacted.

This request does not seek unrelated personnel files or personal financial information.
