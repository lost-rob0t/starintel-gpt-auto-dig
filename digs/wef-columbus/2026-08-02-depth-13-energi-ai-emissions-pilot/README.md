## Public pilot scope

Smart Columbus describes a City-funded pilot developed with Energi.AI:

- pilot period: July 2025–July 2026
- up to 50 Franklin County businesses
- six months of free platform access per participant
- dashboards covering electricity, gas, water, waste, costs, and emissions
- recommendations, benchmarking, forecasting, and Scope 1/2 reporting
- optional contribution of anonymized business data to a citywide Community View
- Community View launch announced for November 2025

Named participants include OhioHealth, Huntington, Installed Building Products, Bread Financial, and The Ohio State University.
