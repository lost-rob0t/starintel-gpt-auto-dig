# Vance–Rockbridge anti-fraud–Palantir timeline

**Root dataset:** `palantir-deep-dive-2026-07-25`  
**Research cutoff:** July 31, 2026  

## Why this branch matters

This is the closest public-record chain yet connecting:

`private Rockbridge donor access`
→ `a Vance-controlled official program`
→ `government adoption of Palantir software`
→ `economic and institutional benefit to a company chaired by Peter Thiel`

It does **not** yet prove that Rockbridge or Thiel privately ordered the Palantir deployments. It does establish a narrow causal window that now warrants procurement-file, calendar, and communications acquisition.

## What is proved

- Vance chairs the task force.
- The task force is designed around large-scale data sharing, identity and eligibility verification, risk scoring, cross-program indicators, and vendor-supported technical operations.
- Vance privately briefed Rockbridge's high-dollar donor network on the task force fourteen days after its creation.
- A USDA Palantir fraud pilot began seven days after that summit.
- SBA later publicly tied an expanded Palantir fraud deployment to partnership with Vance's task force.
- Peter Thiel remained Palantir's chairman and a director in 2026.

## What remains unproved

- Thiel's attendance at the March 30 summit.
- A private request from Thiel, Rockbridge, Huemme, Buskirk, or another intermediary that Palantir be selected.
- Vance's participation in a particular Palantir source-selection, pilot, modification, or expansion decision.
- Whether the USDA and SBA deployments were competed, sole-sourced, initiated before the task force, or independently selected by agency officials.
- Any financial benefit to Vance through Narya arising from Palantir's awards.

## Covert-control hypothesis

This sequence is consistent with a distributed model in which private donor access shapes priorities while aligned personnel and agencies implement them without an explicit written order from the ultimate patron.

The public evidence now supports this bounded claim:

> Vance privately marketed an official data-intensive anti-fraud program to his former dark-money donor network shortly before and during federal adoption of Palantir fraud platforms, producing a direct institutional benefit for the company chaired by his principal political patron.

The evidence does not yet establish why Palantir was selected.
