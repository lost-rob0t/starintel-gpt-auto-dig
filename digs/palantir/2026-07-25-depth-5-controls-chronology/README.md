## Branch A — IRS technical and access controls

The IRS Enterprise Data Platform privacy records document an existing control stack rather than an uncontrolled data lake:

- source data from major taxpayer-account, return, information-reporting and compliance systems;
- identity and entitlement management through BEARS;
- live-data waiver forms for applicable access;
- moderate-risk background investigations for contractor and subcontractor access;
- read-only, administrator, developer and testing roles;
- AWS CloudTrail, AppDynamics and Databricks activity logging;
- Advanced Analytics Platform restrictions against exporting EDP data from the analytical tools;
- Immuta masking of personally identifiable information and filtering of negative-TIN data;
- forwarding of cloud tracking logs to IRS Splunk.

This changes the unresolved question. The question is not whether IRS has any controls. The depth-6 question is whether the new Palantir Unified API order inherited the exact EDP authorization boundary, waiver process, masking rules, service-account governance, application-consumer approvals and audit-retention requirements.

## Branch D — 1789 investment-to-award chronology

Chronology separates the four portfolio cases:

| Company | Cited federal event | 1789 investment announcement | Sequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cerebras Systems | April 2025 military computing project | September 30, 2025 Series G | Award before investment |
| PsiQuantum | April 2025 AFRL contract | September 10, 2025 Series E | Award before investment |
| Firehawk Aerospace | August 25, 2025 Air Force contract | September 24, 2025 1789-led round | Award before investment |
| Vulcan Elements | November 21, 2025 conditional federal loan | August 2025 financing with 1789 participation | Investment before financing |

The first three chronologies weaken the claim that a disclosed 1789 investment produced the cited award because the public investment announcement followed it. Private discussions or earlier unsigned commitments remain unknown, but the disclosed sequence points in the opposite direction.

Vulcan remains the priority branch. The reported 1789 investment preceded the **$620 million conditional** Office of Strategic Capital commitment by approximately three months. The stake remains undisclosed, and the commitment is not represented as a completed disbursement.

## Depth-5 assessment

Depth 5 produces four analytical corrections:

1. **IRS controls exist.** The investigation now concerns control inheritance, implementation and exceptions.
2. **Barbaccia’s disclosure is real, but the ethics conclusion is unresolved.** Public absence is not proof of misconduct or compliance.
3. **Rockbridge is an operating network of distinct entities.** Shared personnel and activity must not be collapsed into one organization or balance sheet.
4. **Chronology eliminates three weak causal theories and elevates one stronger lead.** Cerebras, PsiQuantum and Firehawk were publicly awarded before 1789’s announced investment; Vulcan’s investment came first.

No reviewed evidence establishes that Thiel, Vance, Trump Jr., 1789 Capital or prior Palantir employees directed an award or bypassed a required control.
