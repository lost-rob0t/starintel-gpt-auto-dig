# Trump–Thiel–Vance control test — FEC and NGO pass

**Root dataset:** `palantir-deep-dive-2026-07-25`  
**Research cutoff:** July 31, 2026  
**Branch:** `agent/trump-thiel-vance-fec-ngo-control-test`

## Question

Do campaign-finance, nonprofit, personnel, and policy records prove that JD Vance operates as a covert agent controlled by Peter Thiel?

## Result

**No. The reviewed public record does not prove covert control.**

It does establish a narrower and still material conclusion:

> Peter Thiel and aligned institutions helped incubate Vance's career through employment, venture backing, dominant electoral financing, donor-network infrastructure, personnel development, and access to Trump's political coalition.

That is evidence of **patronage dependence, institutional incubation, and ideological alignment**. A “Manchurian candidate” claim would require evidence of covert instruction, quid pro quo, coercion, concealed agency, blackmail, or foreign direction. None was located in this pass.

## FEC findings

Protect Ohio Values PAC (`C00770495`) reported:

- **$19,797,824.15** in total receipts during the 2022 cycle;
- **$15,985,860.20** in independent expenditures in the FEC committee summary;
- FEC-derived Itemizer data attributes approximately **$14,699,532** in spending supporting JD Vance.

Public reporting and FEC-derived donor normalization identify **$15 million from Peter Thiel across three receipts**. Against the FEC total-receipts figure, that is approximately **75.8%** of the committee's 2022-cycle receipts.

This is unusually concentrated patronage. It is not itself evidence that the PAC or candidate accepted secret commands.

## Coordination allegation and counterevidence

FEC MUR 8009 examined allegations that Protect Ohio Values supplied polling and voter-targeting material to the Vance campaign as an unreported in-kind contribution.

The Commission:

- failed 1–5 to find reason to believe on the Deep Root Analytics theory;
- voted 5–1 to dismiss the relevant allegations;
- closed the matter without establishing a violation.

The enforcement outcome blocks any claim that unlawful coordination was proved. It also does not prove that every interaction alleged in the complaint was factually impossible.

### Personnel pipeline

American Moment describes itself as an IRS-approved 501(c)(3) created to identify, educate, and credential personnel for government and public-policy roles. It identifies Vance as a founding-board adviser and currently advertises programs for Capitol Hill and administration staff.

This is direct evidence of a movement personnel pipeline. It is not proof that Thiel controls its placements or that alumni act as covert agents.
