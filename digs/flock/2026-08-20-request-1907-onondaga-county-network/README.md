# Onondaga County ALPR network: Syracuse is not the county

**Request:** #1907  
**Dataset root:** `digs/flock/`  
**Date:** 2026-08-20

Syracuse removed its municipal Flock readers by July 23, 2026. That does not mean Onondaga County became Flock-free.

Separate control surfaces remain visible in the public record:

- the Onondaga County Sheriff's Office independently documents license-plate-reader use, although the current vendor for that sheriff equipment is unresolved in the official source reviewed here;
- public surveillance-index evidence identifies six Flock readers associated with Onondaga Community College campus safety as of September 2025;
- the same index records East Syracuse Police acquiring two Flock readers in 2023;
- Syracuse separately dealt with a reader owned by the Town of DeWitt but physically located on Syracuse property.

That last case is the useful modeling lesson: **where a camera stands is not who owns the network.**

## Still unresolved

- current sheriff ALPR vendor, inventory and contracts;
- current OCC and East Syracuse deployment status;
- DeWitt inventory and ownership details;
- directed sharing among sheriff, campus, municipal and neighboring systems;
- whether any county/neighboring access survived Syracuse's municipal Flock termination.
