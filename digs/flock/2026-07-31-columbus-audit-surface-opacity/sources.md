## Handling

- Vendor statements, public-record artifacts, reporting, methodologies, and requests are typed separately.
- Probabilistic operator matches are not promoted as verified identities.
- No audit-log field is assumed absent from Flock's internal storage merely because it is absent from a public or customer export.
