## Verification boundary

No direct person-specific board-member email address is published here unless a first-party source intentionally exposes it as a professional contact. Search/broker databases that claim or infer addresses are excluded from this public-contact surface. A missing direct address means **not verified in this pass**, not that no such professional address exists.
