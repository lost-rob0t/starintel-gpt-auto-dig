# Auto-Dig request #1900 — Cornell campus Flock controls

## Findings

- Cornell Daily Sun reporting identifies seven Flock Safety cameras operating at major entry points to central campus since October 2024 under Cornell University Police Department context.
- Cornell's current Policy 8.1, Physical Security Systems, requires security cameras to be integrated into the university's centralized surveillance system unless exempted and says cameras must be viewable by the Division of Public Safety Communications Center, Cornell University Police Department, and Access Control Program.
- The Access Control Program describes itself as the Division of Public Safety unit administering centralized video-surveillance services and Policy 8.1 compliance tooling.
- Faculty Senate Resolution 198 passed December 20, 2024 and demanded public information and governance consultation around increased police/security presence and camera deployment; the archive also records an administration response dated February 10, 2025.
- These policy and governance records establish Cornell-wide control requirements and oversight context. They do not, by themselves, prove that the seven Flock cameras were technically integrated into the centralized system, that every named unit had Flock-platform credentials, or that any Ithaca Police sharing edge existed.
