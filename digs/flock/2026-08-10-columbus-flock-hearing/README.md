# Columbus Flock hearing follow-up — 2026-08-10

This packet records the August 10, 2026 Columbus City Council Public Safety & Criminal Justice Committee hearing on Flock Safety cameras and the Columbus Division of Police audit.

## Findings

Columbus Government Television lists **“Public Safety & Criminal Justice Cmte: Flock Safety Cameras Audit Review”** in the official Council Committees catalog. WOSU reported the hearing was scheduled for **August 10, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. at Columbus City Hall**.

Axios Columbus reported on August 12 that the hearing lasted **nearly six hours**, **55 residents** testified, and the public feedback was overwhelmingly negative. Axios also reported that Columbus police defended Flock as useful for solving crimes and pointed to current sharing restrictions and safeguards.

The strongest concrete follow-up is the contract decision. Columbus Ordinance 3181-2024 authorized **$228,000** for **38 fixed Flock LPRs** under a **two-year term beginning when the purchase order was confirmed/certified**. Axios reports that the current contract reaches a renewal-or-lapse decision point in **December 2026**.

## Current status

No official final minutes, Council vote, renewal decision, resulting legislation, or post-hearing policy action was identified in this pass.

The report that a Flock representative left before public commentary is carried by Axios citing Matter News. It is retained as a gap/caveat rather than promoted to an official-city finding.
