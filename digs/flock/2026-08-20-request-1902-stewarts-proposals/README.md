## Findings

- The Rensselaer County Sheriff's Office approached Stewart's Shops in August 2025 about hosting readers at **five stores in Rensselaer County**.
- By December 2025, Stewart's had declined the proposal. Spokesperson Robin Cooper said customer privacy concerns were part of the decision and questioned whether a private company should host this kind of law-enforcement system rather than government property.
- The proposed readers were described as law-enforcement monitored. Nothing in the reviewed evidence establishes that Stewart's would have owned the cameras, administered Flock accounts, or received plate-query access.
- Hudson Police separately approached Stewart's about a Columbia County site. Stewart's also declined. The exact property and native proposal remain unresolved.
- These are **rejected private-site proposals**, not deployments. They are not folded into the county's reported eight-camera inventory without ownership/deployment evidence.
