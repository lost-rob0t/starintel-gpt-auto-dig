## Eli Hurvitz: foundation, Rothschild philanthropy, Knesset defense oversight, intelligence, and Meridor family

An Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities report commissioned and funded by the Trump Foundation includes a biographical sketch stating that Eli Hurvitz:

- served as deputy director of Yad Hanadiv from 2000 to 2011;
- previously served as assistant to the chair of the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee;
- held several officer roles in Military Intelligence's Central Collection Unit.

The Jerusalem Post identifies Hurvitz as Dan Meridor's nephew. Harvard's Belfer Center records that Dan Meridor became chair of the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee after his 1999 election.

Supported path:

```text
Trump Foundation management
  -> Eli Hurvitz
  + Yad Hanadiv / Rothschild-family philanthropy
  + Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee staff
  + Military Intelligence Central Collection Unit
  + Meridor family
```

### Chairman-identity inference

Dan Meridor is a strong candidate for the unnamed committee chair whom Hurvitz assisted because:

1. Dan Meridor became committee chair in 1999.
2. Hurvitz moved to Yad Hanadiv in 2000.
3. Hurvitz is Meridor's nephew.

This remains an **inference**, not a resolved employment fact. No reviewed primary personnel record names the chair. The depth-8 pass must retrieve Knesset staff rosters, payroll or appointment records, and office archives before creating a direct `worked_for` relation.
