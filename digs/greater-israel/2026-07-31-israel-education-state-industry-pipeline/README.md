## Correction

The **Eddie and Jules Trump Family Foundation is unrelated to Donald Trump as an organization**. That does **not** make it unrelated to the existing Greater Israel dataset.

It belongs in this dataset because official foundation records expose a cross-sector Israeli education and technical-talent network linking:

```text
Eddie and Jules Trump Family Foundation
  + Rashi Foundation
  + Intel Israel
        |
        v
      Sheatufim
        |
        v
  5p2 national coalition
        |
        +-- Israel Ministry of Education
        +-- Weizmann Institute of Science
        +-- Technion
        +-- Amal School Network
        +-- technology companies
        +-- Ministry of Defense R&D representation
```

The foundation's own strategic background also describes an education funnel:

```text
middle-school excellence classes
  -> five-unit high-school mathematics
  -> IDF elite technological units
  -> university engineering
  -> Israeli high-tech, science and medicine
```

This is a **state-capacity and technical-human-capital pipeline**, not a Donald Trump business relation.

## Source-backed findings

### Foundation role

The Eddie and Jules Trump Family Foundation was created in 2011 to expand excellence in mathematics and science within Israel's education system.

### 5p2 founding network

The foundation's 2013 grant record states that it partnered with:

- Rashi Foundation;
- Intel Israel.

The three partners asked Sheatufim to convene a cross-sector national coalition on science and mathematics education. The grant amount recorded for this phase was **NIS 750,000**.

### Government, defense and intelligence participation

The official record describes a coalition workshop attended by sixty organizational representatives, including:

- the Minister of Education;
- the vice president of the Weizmann Institute;
- the Technion's head of Science Education;
- the Amal School Network;
- representatives associated with Qualcomm, SanDisk, Teva and Cisco;
- the Jerusalem Science Museum;
- a representative of research and development at the Ministry of Defense;
- officers from Military Intelligence.

The record establishes participation. It does not establish that defense or Military Intelligence controlled the coalition.

### National-program expansion

A later foundation retrospective says:

- almost 100 organizations joined 5p2;
- the initiative sought to double the number of high-school graduates completing five-unit mathematics, science and technology;
- the coalition developed into a national program.

The exact governance vehicle, government agreements and program ownership after adoption remain unresolved.

## Assessment

The supported model is:

```text
philanthropic capital
  -> industry partnership
  -> nonprofit convening
  -> government policy alignment
  -> school and university implementation
  -> technical talent production
  -> military-technology and high-tech pathways
```

This network is relevant to Greater Israel research because it documents an institutional mechanism for developing scientific, technical, military and industrial capacity.

The evidence does **not** establish:

- an organizational relationship with Donald Trump;
- military control of Israeli mathematics education;
- compulsory recruitment into IDF technology units;
- that every coalition participant shared one political or territorial objective.
