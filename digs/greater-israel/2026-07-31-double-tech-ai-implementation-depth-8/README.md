## Double Tech

MAOZ's official launch post says Double Tech is co-led with the Eddie and Jules Trump Family Foundation and the Israel Ministry of Education. Its stated objective is to double the number of students completing high-tech matriculation.

The launch convened approximately:

- 260 participants;
- 22 working tables;
- government;
- the education system;
- local authorities;
- high-tech industry;
- academia;
- philanthropy;
- civil society.

MAOZ separately frames technological human capital as part of national security and economic-social resilience.

## Policy-to-implementation chain

The government-appointed human-capital committee chaired by Dadi Perlmutter used Aharon Institute research. Foundation material states that the committee's education recommendations were adopted and authorized for execution.

The government target described in the foundation's 2024 consultation was:

- 15% of high-school graduates;
- approximately 20,000 students;
- by 2028;
- with dedicated targets for female students, Arab students and students in peripheral regions;
- implemented using a collective-impact model.

The exact target differs across later foundation grant pages, some of which describe 17%. The primary government decision and amendments remain a priority record gap.

## National AI layer

Current government records identify Erez Askal as director of the National AI Directorate in the Prime Minister's Office.

The July 2026 national plan includes:

- 100,000 AI processors;
- national AI and quantum-computing infrastructure;
- AI literacy beginning in eighth grade in September 2026;
- new university AI programs;
- workforce training;
- application of AI to education, healthcare and transportation;
- explicit national-security framing.

The Directorate also began consultations with high-tech companies regarding national AI infrastructure and implementation.

MAOZ's Double Tech launch communication tagged the Directorate and Erez Askal. A secondary participant post quotes Askal speaking at the conference. This packet records the connection conservatively: **the Directorate is adjacent to the launch and human-capital policy, but no reviewed source establishes a formal Double Tech governance role.**
