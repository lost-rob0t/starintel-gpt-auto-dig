## The “failed school” claim

The evidence indicates a likely conflation between two unrelated entities:

1. **Trump University** was a U.S. real-estate seminar and mentoring company founded by Donald Trump, Michael Sexton and another person. New York courts described it as unlicensed and later renamed Trump Entrepreneur Initiative. No reviewed source connects it to Netanyahu or an Israeli school.
2. The **Eddie and Jules Trump Family Foundation** is an Israeli education philanthropy founded in 2011. It works with the Israeli education system and partnered with the Ministry of Education, the Rashi Foundation and Intel Israel on mathematics and science programs. The foundation is named for Eddie and Jules Trump, not Donald Trump.

The Israeli foundation is a surname collision, not evidence that Donald Trump and Netanyahu operated a school together.

## Main findings

### 1. Ramat Gan was a real licensing dispute, not a Netanyahu venture

Trump Marks licensed the Trump name for a proposed 70-storey tower on the former Elite factory site in Ramat Gan. Reporting on the New York judgment says Trump claimed 25 percent of apartment-sale proceeds plus royalties. Crescent Heights sold the land to Azorim rather than building the project, and the court ruled the agreement neither required construction nor prohibited a land sale.

This is the strongest evidence of a legally structured Trump-linked Israeli property deal. The identified commercial counterparty was Crescent Heights, not Netanyahu.

### 2. Netanya was advanced through Michael Dezer

Netanya Mayor Miriam Feirberg described meeting Michael Dezer, Trump's Miami development partner, and later meeting Trump. Trump expressed interest in a beachfront hotel, but the project remained on paper.

The available record supports a proposed development pathway:

`Netanya municipal promotion -> Michael Dezer -> Donald Trump brand/project interest`

It does not support a Trump–Netanyahu joint venture.

### 3. Ashkelon was a proposed golf-resort complex

David Friedman, then Trump's lawyer and later U.S. ambassador to Israel, inspected and promoted an Ashkelon site for a Trump golf course and resort. The described plan included golf, hotels, a convention center and commercial facilities. No completed project was identified.

Again, the visible network runs through Friedman, municipal actors and land authorities—not Netanyahu as a commercial partner.

### 4. Trump Drinks Israel was implemented

After trademark litigation, Trump Marks and H. Pixel reached a settlement and licensing arrangement covering Trump-branded vodka and energy drinks in Israel and the Palestinian territories. This is the best-supported completed Israeli commercial arrangement in the packet.

The remaining gaps concern company ownership, related entity names, exact effective dates and termination.

### 5. Trump endorsed Netanyahu, but politics is not a joint venture

Trump released a 2013 video endorsing Netanyahu. Jonny Daniels was publicly linked to producing or arranging it. Contemporaneous accounts conflict: one says Daniels initiated the approach; another quotes Trump saying Netanyahu asked him directly.

That contradiction is worth resolving, but the endorsement remains a political communications edge, not commercial joint ownership.
