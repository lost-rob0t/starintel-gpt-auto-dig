## Findings

Axiom's current public material describes a Republican full-service political consulting operation spanning campaign direction, finance, data/research, voter contact, direct mail, television, digital/OTT, media buying, field services, phones/texting, rapid response, targeting and analytics.

The current team page identifies Jeff Roe as founder and CEO, Rob Phillips as president, and Sarah Hoeller as COO. It also says Axiom has grown into a portfolio of 15 companies. This pass materializes a bounded subset of named portfolio firms as distinct organization nodes instead of flattening them into one Axiom record.

The first portfolio slice includes Madison McQueen, AxCapital, Fundraising Inc, AxMedia, Rockwell Solutions, Vanguard, DVC and Garrison Management. Current public leadership is materialized for Owen Brennan / Madison McQueen, Thomas Datwyler / AxCapital, Alexandra Kingston / Fundraising Inc, Brad Mont / AxMedia, and Scott O'Donnell / Rockwell Solutions.

The graph preserves functional distinctions. AxCapital is represented around campaign-finance/compliance; Fundraising Inc around fundraising; AxMedia around media buying and distribution; Rockwell Solutions around direct response/direct mail; Vanguard around field/canvassing; and DVC around political creative/direct-mail work. These are organizational/function observations from the firm's current public team and service descriptions, not claims that every Axiom client uses every service.

Axiom's current homepage also exposes the `Axiom News Brief` as a public subscription surface. The current contact page separately discloses optional consent for automated calls and text messages from Axiom Strategies. The packet records the newsletter surface but does not collect subscriber information, private CRM data, or nonpublic delivery records.
