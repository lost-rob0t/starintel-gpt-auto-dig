# Election communications / voter-poll media ecosystem — pass 17

Bounded breadth-first public-web enumeration pass for the `anarchist-violence` corpus.

## New graph surface

This pass opens a new election-information distribution cluster around **The Voter Poll**, the survey/data product launched for the 2025 general election and used for 2026 election coverage. First-party AP and SSRS material identifies AP, ABC News, CBS News, CNN, FOX News Media, NBC News and MSNOW as participants/users; AP states SSRS conducts the survey. SSRS separately documents its 2025 acquisition of Edison Research, while Verasight publicly identifies itself as a primary data contributor to The Voter Poll.

AP also states that ABC News, CBS News, CNN, NBC News and FOX News Media receive AP U.S. election-results data, creating a second information-distribution layer adjacent to the survey consortium.

## Coverage

Materialized canonical v0.9 packet records for sources, organizations, public people, the survey/media surface, explicit relations, and one recursive investigation target. Relations are limited to claims directly supported by the cited public source; no ownership, coordination, editorial-control, or identity inference is asserted beyond source evidence.

The strongest recursive frontier is downstream distribution: AP says The Voter Poll data is available beyond the consortium to media and non-media users. A follow-on pass should enumerate named downstream customers, API/graphic distribution paths, voter-file/panel suppliers, methodological partners, and election-night syndication edges.
