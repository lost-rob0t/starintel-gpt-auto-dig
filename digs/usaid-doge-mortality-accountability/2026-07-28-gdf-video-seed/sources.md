## Classification rules

- Government orders and congressional research establish observed actions and formal authority.
- Peer-reviewed and institutional models remain model-derived estimates.
- Named-case reporting establishes reported deaths, service interruptions, and interview testimony; it does not automatically establish but-for causation.
- Interviews, public statements, legal positions, and video titles are attributed claims or rhetoric.
