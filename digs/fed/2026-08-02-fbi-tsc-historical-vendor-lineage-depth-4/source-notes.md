## Evidence rules

- Preserve requirement scope and dates.
- Do not collapse operational, IT, facilities, analytic and AI-enhancement work.
- Do not convert incumbency into current-team status.
- Do not convert a protest representative into proposal or program leadership.
- Do not infer an AI-RFI response from prior TSC work.
