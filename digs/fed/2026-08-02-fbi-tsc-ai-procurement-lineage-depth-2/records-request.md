# Records request — FBI-TSC-AIE respondents, evaluation and briefings

## Subject

Records sufficient to identify entities and personnel participating in the FBI Threat Screening Center AI Enhancement market research, notice `FBI-TSC-AIE`.

## Date range

March 1, 2026 through the date of search.

## Requested records

1. The response-receipt log for `FBI-TSC-AIE`.
2. Email metadata sufficient to identify each response:
   - sender organization;
   - sender name and official business address;
   - received date and time;
   - subject line;
   - attachment names and file types.
3. The legal or DBA name, UEI, CAGE code, business size, GSA Schedule, SIN, NASA SEWP contract and authorized reseller identified by each respondent.
4. The respondent point of contact and named key personnel.
5. The market-research report, sources-sought summary, acquisition-strategy input, vendor list, capability matrix, scoring sheet, ranking, evaluator notes and recommendation.
6. Invitations to brief or demonstrate, schedules, attendee lists, presentation titles and participating company personnel.
7. Questions, clarifications, meeting notes and post-RFI communications.
8. Records identifying:
   - the TSC Information Technology Unit requirement owner;
   - acquisition planner;
   - contracting specialist;
   - program manager;
   - technical evaluators;
   - AI Ethics Council reviewers or approvers;
   - officials who drafted, approved, amended, archived or reposted the RFI.
9. Any follow-on:
   - procurement request;
   - statement of objectives or work;
   - solicitation;
   - task order;
   - award;
   - modification;
   - cancellation;
   - decision not to proceed.

## Segregability

Proprietary technical content, pricing detail, classified information and protected personal information may be redacted. Please release segregable metadata sufficient to establish:

- respondent identity;
- public business identifiers;
- receipt date;
- document title;
- government author;
- evaluator identity and official role;
- briefing participant identity and official role;
- disposition of the market research.

## Preferred format

Searchable electronic records, including native spreadsheets where available.
