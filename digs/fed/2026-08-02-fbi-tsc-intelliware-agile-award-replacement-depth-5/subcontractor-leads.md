## Rejected inference paths

Do not classify a company as a TSC subcontractor merely because it:

- reacted to or reposted the award;
- holds FBI ITSSS-2;
- has an intelligence-analysis capability statement;
- employs a former FBI official;
- holds a GSA schedule compatible with the requirement;
- appears in the same social-network cluster;
- has worked with Agile Defense, IntelliWare, IntelliBridge or BAE on a different contract.

## Required proof

Promote a current subcontractor only with one or more of:

- official prime or subcontractor announcement naming the TSC award;
- federal first-tier subaward record;
- approved subcontracting plan or consent-to-subcontract record;
- proposal/team roster released by the government;
- public job posting that names the prime, teammate and TSC award;
- named employee statement explicitly tying the company to `15F06726F0000362`;
- invoice, modification or transition document identifying the subcontractor.
