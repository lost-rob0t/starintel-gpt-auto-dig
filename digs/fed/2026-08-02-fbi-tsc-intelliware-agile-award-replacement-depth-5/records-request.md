# Records request — TSC Intelligence Analysis Services award sequence

## Subject

Records sufficient to explain the acquisition sequence and identify participating entities and personnel for FBI Threat Screening Center Intelligence Analysis Services awards:

- `15F06725F0001209` — BAE Systems Technology Solutions & Services Inc.;
- `15F06725F0001838` — BAE bridge order;
- `15F06726F0000362` — IntelliWare Systems, Inc.

## Date range

January 1, 2024 through the date of search.

## Requested records

1. The solicitation, task-order request, statement of work, performance work statement, amendments, questions and answers, evaluation plan and acquisition timeline associated with each award.
2. Records identifying the relationship among the three award actions, including:
   - bridge justification;
   - award suspension;
   - corrective action;
   - cancellation or rescission;
   - termination notice;
   - deobligation decision;
   - transition instruction;
   - reevaluation or reopened competition;
   - replacement-award memorandum;
   - decision not to proceed with an earlier award.
3. The offer-receipt log for each competition, sufficient to identify:
   - legal and DBA names;
   - UEI and CAGE code;
   - GSA MAS contract or parent IDV;
   - prime, joint-venture, teaming and subcontract status;
   - date and time received;
   - offeror point of contact.
4. Unsuccessful-offeror notices and debriefing schedules, excluding technical and pricing content where necessary.
5. Source-selection records sufficient to identify:
   - source-selection authority;
   - contracting officer and specialist;
   - technical evaluation board members;
   - past-performance evaluators;
   - security evaluators;
   - price evaluators;
   - final ranking and award recommendation.
6. Protest and litigation records, including agency-level protests, GAO protests, Court of Federal Claims actions, voluntary corrective-action notices, stays and dismissal records.
7. Contract modification and transaction records explaining every obligation and deobligation affecting the BAE long-term order, BAE bridge and IntelliWare order.
8. BAE and IntelliWare/Agile records identifying:
   - named key personnel;
   - program and deputy program managers;
   - transition manager;
   - quality-control manager;
   - security manager;
   - subcontract manager;
   - site and shift leads;
   - analysts or technical specialists designated as key personnel;
   - approved substitutions.
9. Subcontracting plans, consent-to-subcontract records, first-tier subcontract reports, teaming agreements submitted to the FBI, and approved subcontractor lists.
10. Transition-in and transition-out plans, schedules, staffing matrices, incumbent-capture records and government-furnished workforce information.
11. Records cross-referencing any offeror, awardee, subcontractor or named key person with the later `FBI-TSC-AIE` market-research response, briefing or demonstration process.

## Segregability

Technical solutions, classified information, source-selection-sensitive material, proprietary pricing and protected personal information may be redacted. Release segregable metadata sufficient to establish:

- document title and date;
- author and recipient organization;
- official government role;
- offeror identity and public business identifiers;
- award or protest disposition;
- modification number, action date and obligation amount;
- company participant and official business role;
- transition and subcontract status.

## Preferred format

- native spreadsheets for offer and evaluation logs;
- native contract-modification data;
- searchable PDFs for memoranda and decisions;
- email metadata in CSV format;
- machine-readable award and transaction exports.

## Search locations

Search the records of:

- FBI Finance and Facilities Division procurement personnel;
- the Threat Screening Center requirement owner;
- the awarding and funding office identified in federal award records;
- acquisition planning and source-selection repositories;
- protest and Office of General Counsel files;
- contract administration and financial-management systems;
- transition and security offices supporting the TSC.
