# Lutnick participation after the Jan. 16 Cantor engagement

**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** active research

## Objective

Determine what Howard Lutnick personally did in the USA Rare Earth government-financing matter **after** Cantor Fitzgerald and Moelis entered their placement-agent engagements on or about January 16, 2026.

The legal/evidentiary purpose is narrow: separate ceremonial/public involvement from evidence of personal and substantial participation in negotiation, approval, direction, term-setting, or execution after Cantor was formally engaged.

## Bottom line

The public record reviewed so far establishes **strong pre-Jan. 16 origination involvement** by Lutnick but only **limited post-Jan. 16 evidence**.

Confirmed:

- In November 2025, Ken Moelis introduced USA Rare Earth executives to Lutnick.
- Lutnick says he then introduced USAR to the CHIPS team "to pursue the transaction."
- Cantor and Moelis placement-agent engagement letters were dated on or about January 16, 2026.
- On January 26, the government LOI and private PIPE were publicly announced.
- Lutnick was publicly associated with and supportive of the transaction, but Commerce's own January 26 release quotes the operational program officials Michael Grimes and Bill Frauenhofer rather than attributing negotiation or approval to Lutnick.
- The June 3 definitive Commerce instruments were executed by **Bill Frauenhofer, Executive Director, Semiconductor Innovation and Investment**, not by Lutnick.
- The June direct-funding agreement contains no textual reference to Howard Lutnick by name.

Not established publicly yet:

- that Lutnick negotiated terms after January 16;
- that he approved the $277M direct award or $1.3B loan terms;
- that he created or approved the >=$500M non-federal-capital condition;
- that he knew Cantor had been formally engaged when he made any substantive decision;
- that he received deal briefings after January 16;
- that he approved the PIPE, placement-agent selection, or Cantor compensation;
- that he signed or countersigned the final funding instruments; or
- that he was screened out after his initial introduction.

The public evidence therefore supports **origination / referral participation**, but it does not yet establish the depth of post-engagement participation needed to resolve the § 2635.502 question.

## Chronology

### November 2025 — government-side origination

At the April 23, 2026 Commerce budget hearing, Lutnick stated that Ken Moelis introduced USAR executives to him. Lutnick said the integrated mine-to-magnet model was interesting and that he then introduced USAR to the CHIPS team "to pursue the transaction."

This is direct testimony from Lutnick and is the strongest public evidence of his personal role.

**Classification:** substantive origination/referral into the government transaction process.

**Timing:** before Cantor's January 2026 placement-agent engagement.

### On or about January 16, 2026 — Cantor/Moelis placement-agent engagements

USAR's Securities Purchase Agreement identifies separate Cantor and Moelis engagement letters dated on or about January 16.

This is the critical temporal dividing line for the present sub-pass.

The unanswered question is what Lutnick personally did **after this date**, when Cantor's role in the private financing had been formally established.

### January 26, 2026 — LOI / PIPE public announcement

Commerce announced the non-binding USAR LOI for proposed $277 million direct funding plus a $1.3 billion senior secured loan.

The same-day USAR PIPE announcement identified Cantor as lead placement agent and Moelis as co-placement agent.

Commerce's own public release quotes:

- Michael Grimes, Executive Director of the U.S. Investment Accelerator; and
- Bill Frauenhofer, Director of the CHIPS Program.

The release does not attribute the transaction's negotiation, approval, or term-setting to Lutnick.

USAR/public materials associated with the transaction include administration-level support, but such public endorsement is not equivalent to evidence of personal and substantial participation in the underlying matter.

**Classification:** public/ceremonial association; insufficient by itself to establish decision-level participation.

### April 23, 2026 — Lutnick testifies under questioning

Senator Chris Van Hollen asked whether the transaction originated in the November 2025 Lutnick/USAR contact.

Lutnick responded by describing the Moelis introduction and his own referral of USAR to the CHIPS team.

Van Hollen then raised Cantor's role on the private side and asked Lutnick to respond to the outstanding congressional inquiry. Lutnick said he would take another look at the letter and see if they could work together to resolve the issues.

In this exchange, Lutnick did **not** publicly state that he:

- negotiated the award;
- approved its terms;
- chose Cantor;
- approved Cantor's role;
- approved the non-federal-capital condition; or
- recused himself after the referral.

The exchange therefore confirms origination but leaves the post-Jan. 16 participation depth unresolved.

### June 3, 2026 — definitive agreements executed

USAR and Commerce executed the Direct Funding Agreement, Loan Guarantee Agreement, Securities Issuance Agreement, and related warrant documents.

The SEC-filed Direct Funding Agreement states that Commerce is an agency acting through the Secretary of Commerce **or an appropriate authorized representative**.

The actual Commerce signature block is:

- **Bill Frauenhofer**
- Executive Director, Semiconductor Innovation and Investment

The Securities Issuance Agreement likewise was signed for Commerce by Frauenhofer.

A search of the filed Direct Funding Agreement finds **no occurrence of "Howard Lutnick."**

**Classification:** final execution authority was exercised by an authorized program official, not by Lutnick personally.

### What final signature evidence does and does not prove

It supports:

- Frauenhofer was the executing Commerce official on the definitive instrument;
- Lutnick was not the named final signatory.

It does **not** prove:

- Lutnick lacked upstream approval authority;
- Lutnick did not receive briefings;
- Lutnick did not direct staff;
- Lutnick did not approve policy/transaction parameters before signature; or
- Lutnick was formally recused.

Those require internal approval-chain and ethics-screening records.

## Decision-chain evidence from final instruments

The June Direct Funding Agreement is useful because it makes the operational delegation visible.

It says the Department acts through the Secretary or an appropriate authorized representative, but the execution page names **Bill Frauenhofer**.

This suggests the operative program office held delegated execution authority for the award.

That is **not** enough to infer that every prior approval occurred below the Secretary. The next evidence target is the internal approval chain preceding Frauenhofer's signature.

## Highest-value missing records

1. **Jan. 16-January 28 Lutnick calendar and briefing log** for USAR / CHIPS / critical-minerals meetings.
2. **Commerce deal approval memorandum** identifying required concurrence/approval levels for the USAR LOI.
3. **CHIPS Investment Committee or equivalent recommendation materials** for USAR.
4. Any **Secretary decision memorandum**, action memo, routing slip, concurrence sheet, or approval email for the LOI.
5. Records showing who created and approved the **>= $500M non-federal capital condition**.
6. Records showing when Lutnick or his immediate staff first learned Cantor had been retained for the PIPE.
7. Emails or messages from Lutnick or Office of the Secretary staff giving substantive transaction instructions after Jan. 16.
8. Briefing materials provided to Lutnick before the Jan. 26 announcement.
9. Any recusal/screening notice sent to the CHIPS deal team concerning Lutnick after Cantor's role became known.
10. Delegation-of-authority records under which Frauenhofer signed the June agreements.
11. Any Secretary-level concurrence required before Frauenhofer's execution.
12. Questions for the record / supplemental congressional answers from the April 23 hearing addressing USAR.

## Search conclusion for this sub-pass

The public record currently supports a **two-stage model**:

`Lutnick personally opens the door in Nov. 2025 -> CHIPS/program officials operate and execute the transaction`

What is missing is the middle:

`Jan. 16 Cantor engagement -> Jan. 26 LOI -> June finalization`

No reviewed public source yet shows whether Lutnick remained substantively involved in that middle period or was screened/delegated out.

This missing middle is now the highest-value participation evidence gap.

## Current classification

**Confirmed:** Lutnick personally referred USAR into the CHIPS process before Cantor's January engagement; program officials Frauenhofer/Grimes publicly fronted the Jan. 26 transaction; Frauenhofer executed the June definitive agreement for Commerce.

**Unresolved:** whether Lutnick personally and substantially participated after Cantor's Jan. 16 engagement but before program-level execution, including any approvals, substantive instructions, briefings, or transaction decisions.

**No ethics violation is concluded from this public record.**
