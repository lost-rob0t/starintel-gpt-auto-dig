# 5 C.F.R. § 2635.502 — USAR / Cantor matter-classification test

**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** analytic legal framework based on OGE guidance; not a legal conclusion

## Objective

Define the evidence test for deciding whether Howard Lutnick's former-employer restriction was implicated by his participation in the USA Rare Earth government-financing process while Cantor Fitzgerald was USAR's existing and later lead placement agent.

This report does **not** determine that a violation occurred. It isolates what must be shown or disproved.

## Core rule structure

The relevant ethics-agreement language, as reproduced in official congressional correspondence, tracked 5 C.F.R. § 2635.502: for approximately one year after leaving Cantor, Lutnick agreed not to participate personally and substantially in a **particular matter involving specific parties** in which Cantor or a subsidiary was a **party or represented a party**, absent the applicable authorization mechanism.

That produces three separate factual/legal predicates:

1. **matter predicate** — identify the relevant particular matter involving specific parties;
2. **Cantor predicate** — determine whether Cantor was a party or represented a party in that matter; and
3. **participation predicate** — determine whether Lutnick participated personally and substantially in that matter without a valid authorization.

All three need evidence. A financial benefit to Cantor alone is not enough to answer the § 2635.502 question.

## OGE: what counts as a particular matter involving specific parties

OGE's DAEOgram DO-06-029 describes this as the narrowest "particular matter" category, focused on identified parties. OGE explains that it typically involves:

- a specific proceeding affecting parties' legal rights; or
- an **isolatable transaction or related set of transactions between identified parties**.

OGE gives examples including:

- contracts;
- grants;
- licenses;
- product-approval applications;
- investigations; and
- litigation.

This is directly relevant to USAR because the Commerce process culminated in direct-funding, loan, securities-issuance and warrant agreements between identified parties.

## OGE: a government agreement can become a specific-party matter early

DO-06-029 further states that matters involving government contracts, grants and other agreements can become particular matters involving specific parties before a final agreement exists.

OGE's general rule is that specific parties are first identified when the government receives an expression of interest from a prospective contractor, grantee or other party. OGE also says a matter can involve specific parties even earlier if there are sufficient indications that the government has actually identified the party.

### Application to USAR chronology

The public record says:

`Ken Moelis introduces USAR executives to Lutnick -> Lutnick introduces USAR to CHIPS team -> Commerce/USAR negotiations -> Jan. 26 LOI -> June definitive agreements`

That chronology makes it unreasonable to assume the particular matter began only when definitive documents were signed in June.

The **November 2025 introduction / ensuing USAR-specific Commerce engagement** is a plausible starting point for the particular-matter analysis.

That is an analytic application of OGE guidance, not a finding that ELPO selected that exact start date.

## Covered relationship does not automatically answer the issue

OGE precedents interpreting § 2635.502 make an important distinction: having a covered relationship with a person/entity does not automatically trigger the specific-party recusal provision merely because that person has some financial interest in a matter.

The core § 2635.502(a) question is whether the covered person/entity is **a party or represents a party** in the relevant particular matter.

This is crucial here because Cantor's economic benefit from the PIPE does not, standing alone, establish that Cantor was a party to or representative in **the Commerce-USAR government financing matter**.

## The hardest question: one related set of transactions or two separate matters?

The public transaction architecture creates two competing classifications.

### Classification A — integrated / related set of transactions

Evidence supporting an integrated view:

- Commerce and USAR were negotiating a specific federal funding/loan/equity package.
- USAR disclosed that raising at least $500 million of non-federal capital was a condition/milestone on the path to definitive government financing documents.
- USAR raised $1.5 billion in a Cantor-led PIPE that satisfied that condition.
- The government LOI and PIPE were announced on the same day.
- PIPE purchasers received an investor presentation containing detailed government-transaction economics.
- Moelis served both as USAR's exclusive financial adviser for the government agreements and co-placement agent on the PIPE.

OGE's phrase **"isolatable transaction or related set of transactions"** makes this integration evidence legally relevant to the ethics classification.

### Classification B — legally distinct matters

Evidence supporting separation:

- The federal agreements were between USAR and the U.S. government / Commerce-related parties.
- The PIPE was a private securities transaction between USAR and institutional purchasers.
- Cantor and Moelis were placement agents for the private placement; Cantor was not a signatory to the federal funding/loan agreements identified in the reviewed public documents.
- Cantor had an established USAR placement-agent relationship before the Commerce process.
- Moelis, not Cantor, was identified as USAR's exclusive financial adviser for structuring the government agreements.

A Commerce ethics official could potentially have treated the government transaction and private financing as separate particular matters even though one was economically conditional on the other.

The public record does not yet reveal which classification ELPO adopted.

## Cantor predicate: "party or represents a party"

### Party

Current public documents do not show Cantor as a direct contracting party to the Commerce-USAR direct-funding or loan agreements.

That weakens the most straightforward "Cantor was a party to the government agreement" theory.

### Represents a party

The harder question is whether Cantor's lead-placement-agent role constituted representation of USAR **in the same particular matter**.

Evidence relevant to that question:

- Cantor acted for USAR in raising the private capital needed to satisfy a disclosed government-financing condition.
- The placement-agent engagement letters and fee schedules are separate and currently unlocated.
- Moelis had a dual role: exclusive financial adviser on the government agreements and co-placement agent on the PIPE.
- Cantor's scope of engagement beyond private placement has not been publicly established.

The engagement letter is therefore not merely a fee document; it may define **what Cantor was retained to do and whom it represented for which transaction workstream**.

## Personal and substantial participation predicate

The public record already establishes some Lutnick participation:

- Lutnick says Ken Moelis introduced USAR executives to him.
- Lutnick says he then introduced USAR to the CHIPS team.
- Lutnick publicly endorsed the transaction when the January 26 LOI was announced.

What is still missing:

- whether he directed terms;
- approved milestones;
- selected or approved financing structure;
- participated in negotiations;
- approved the >=$500M non-federal capital condition;
- received briefings after Cantor's January engagement;
- made decisions after being advised that Cantor was lead placement agent; or
- was screened out of substantive transaction stages after the introduction.

The deal-team decision log, calendars, briefing notes and email trail matter more than public ceremonial involvement.

## Authorization predicate

OGE identifies a written authorization mechanism under **5 C.F.R. § 2635.502(d)** when an agency determines participation serves the government's interests notwithstanding an appearance/impartiality issue.

OGE has historically released such authorization records through FOIA / its Electronic Reading Room.

No USAR/Cantor-specific authorization has been located in ordinary public-source searches as of the research cutoff.

This is not proof none exists.

A valid authorization, if one exists, should be examined for:

- date;
- approving official;
- exact matter described;
- facts known about Cantor's role;
- scope of permitted participation;
- conditions or screening limits; and
- whether the authorization predates the relevant Lutnick participation.

## Highest-value documents after applying this test

1. **Jan. 16 Cantor engagement letter** — defines Cantor's scope/representational role.
2. **Commerce ELPO matter-classification memo** — same vs. separate transaction determination.
3. **Any § 2635.502(d) authorization** — scope/date/factual basis.
4. **Gatekeeping/screening agreement + covered-entity lists**.
5. **CHIPS/USAR intake record** — establishes when USAR became an identified specific party.

## Current classification

The government-side USAR process is strongly consistent with OGE's concept of a particular matter involving specific parties, and OGE guidance supports analyzing its beginning well before final June agreements. The unresolved issue is whether Cantor's private-placement representation was part of that same matter or a legally/ethically separate matter. That question, plus the depth of Lutnick's participation and any written authorization, determines the § 2635.502 analysis.

**No ethics violation is concluded from the current public record.**
