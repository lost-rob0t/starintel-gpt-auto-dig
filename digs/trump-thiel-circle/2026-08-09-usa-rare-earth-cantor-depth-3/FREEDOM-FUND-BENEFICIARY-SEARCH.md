# Freedom Fund beneficiary / control roster search

**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** active; public-source boundary documented

## Objective

Identify the natural persons and entities behind **Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP**, including:

- investment decision-makers;
- general-partner and special-limited-partner economics;
- limited partners;
- recipients of the February 2026 pro-rata share distribution;
- the unnamed third investment-committee member; and
- any person whose role creates a source-backed connection to the USA Rare Earth / Commerce / Cantor transaction.

Source-backed roles:

## Unnamed third investment-committee member

USAR's filings identify a three-person committee:

1. Michael Blitzer;
2. Kevin Shannon; and
3. one unnamed person described as having no relationship with USAR or its predecessors/affiliates during the preceding three years.

Equivalent disclosure language appears in other Inflection Point-associated issuer filings. The third person remains unnamed in the reviewed SEC record.

### Candidate controls

No person should be promoted to the third seat without a direct source.

**Adam Saks** remains a lead only because he became IPAM CFO in January 2026 around the governance-change period. No SEC source reviewed says he was the third committee member.

Do not graph Saks as committee member absent explicit evidence.

## USAR insider-distribution audit

The SEC's USA Rare Earth filing index shows one Form 4 filed February 17 with a February 12 transaction: **Kronenfeld's Freedom Fund distribution**.

No second USAR officer/director Form 4 was found for another Freedom Fund distribution in the immediate post-registration window.

### What this rules out

It materially reduces the likelihood that another then-current USAR Section 16 officer/director received a reportable Freedom Fund distribution on February 12 without a corresponding Form 4.

### What it does not rule out

It does not identify or exclude:

- non-insider LPs;
- family offices;
- trusts;
- institutional LPs;
- persons below beneficial-ownership/reporting thresholds;
- persons who received shares through entities;
- recipients whose distribution was not a Section 16 reportable transaction.

## Public-source LP search result

Targeted SEC searches using the exact Freedom Fund name plus:

- `limited partner`;
- `pro-rata distribution`;
- `distribution`;
- `Form 4`; and
- related-party disclosure language

have so far identified **Kronenfeld as the only specifically named Freedom Fund LP**.

This is a **negative-source result, not proof that he was the only LP**.

The approximately $300 million initial subscription itself makes a multi-LP or institutional-capital structure plausible, but the roster cannot be inferred from size.

## Keep Fund I separate

SEC filings for **Inflection Point Fund I, LP** expose a broader set of economic interests:

- Michael Blitzer — performance allocations, management fees, LP interests;
- Kevin Shannon — performance allocations, management fees, LP interests;
- Andrew Gundlach and/or affiliates — performance allocations, management fees, LP interests.

Those disclosures are useful for mapping the broader Inflection Point capital ecosystem.

They **do not establish** that Gundlach, his affiliates, or any other Fund I participant:

- invested in Freedom Fund;
- sat on Freedom Fund's investment committee;
- belonged to Freedom Fund GP/SLP; or
- received USAR PIPE shares from Freedom Fund.

No cross-fund identity should be inferred without documentary support.

## Governance nuance

The January 1, 2026 administrative governance amendment changed beneficial-ownership reporting and moved specified fund-security voting/dispositive decisions into a three-person majority committee.

Later SEC filings still state that **Blitzer controls IPAM** in other sponsor/entity contexts.

Therefore:

- do not describe the January amendment as Blitzer relinquishing company-level control of IPAM;
- describe it as a **fund-level decision-authority restructuring** unless the underlying governance documents establish more.
