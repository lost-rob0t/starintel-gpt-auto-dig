# USAR deal origination and advisor chronology

**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09

## Question

Who brought USA Rare Earth into the Commerce process, when did Cantor become a financing agent, and does the public chronology support or weaken the hypothesis that Commerce or Howard Lutnick steered USAR to Cantor?

## Current finding

The public chronology currently **weakens a simple steering hypothesis**.

Cantor's relationship with USAR predates the November 2025 Commerce introduction and includes a lead-placement-agent role on a $75 million USAR PIPE announced in April 2025. In the April 23, 2026 Senate budget hearing, Lutnick stated that Ken Moelis introduced USAR executives to him and that he then introduced them to the CHIPS team. USAR's January 2026 disclosures identify Moelis & Company as its exclusive financial adviser in structuring the U.S.-government agreements, while Cantor was lead placement agent and Moelis co-placement agent on the linked $1.5 billion PIPE.

That does not resolve the conflict inquiry. It changes its strongest formulation from **"Did Lutnick steer USAR to Cantor?"** to **"Did Lutnick participate in a specific-party government matter during his former-employer restriction while the financing condition caused an existing Cantor client relationship to generate new business for Cantor?"**

## Chronology

### 2023-2024 — predecessor-SPAC relationship

Cantor had underwriting/deferred-fee economics tied to the predecessor SPAC before Howard Lutnick entered Commerce. The exact payment history is being reconciled separately.

### April 29–May 5, 2025 — $75M PIPE

USAR entered a $75 million PIPE with a single institutional investor. SEC-filed company disclosures identify Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. as **lead placement agent**. Cohen Capital Markets also served as a placement agent; other advisers were identified in the closing materials.

This occurred roughly six months before the reported November 2025 USAR/Commerce introduction.

**Implication:** Cantor did not first appear as USAR's placement agent because of the January 2026 Commerce transaction.

### November 2025 — Ken Moelis introduces USAR to Lutnick

At an April 23, 2026 congressional budget hearing, Lutnick stated that Ken Moelis introduced USA Rare Earth executives to him. Lutnick further stated that he introduced the company to the CHIPS team to pursue a transaction.

This is direct testimony establishing Lutnick's role in originating the government-side process.

**What it does not establish:** that Lutnick introduced USAR to Cantor, selected Cantor, negotiated Cantor's fee, or participated in the PIPE.

### January 2026 — dual-advisor structure

USAR's Jan. 26 transaction announcement states:

- Moelis & Company served as exclusive financial adviser to USAR in structuring its agreements with the U.S. government.
- Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. served as lead placement agent for the $1.5 billion PIPE.
- Moelis also served as co-placement agent for that PIPE.

SEC-filed transaction documents state that the Cantor/Moelis placement-agent engagement letters were dated on or about Jan. 16, 2026.

## Network as currently supported

`Ken Moelis -> introduces USAR to Howard Lutnick -> Lutnick introduces USAR to CHIPS team`

`Moelis & Company -> exclusive financial adviser -> USAR government agreements`

`Commerce process -> requires >= $500M non-federal capital -> USAR raises $1.5B PIPE`

`Cantor -> lead placement agent -> $1.5B PIPE`

`Moelis -> co-placement agent -> $1.5B PIPE`

`Cantor -> prior lead placement agent -> $75M USAR PIPE (Apr-May 2025)`

## Hypotheses status

### H1 — Commerce/Lutnick introduced USAR to Cantor

**Current status: weakened / unsupported by reviewed public chronology.**

Reason: Cantor was already acting as USAR's lead placement agent by April 2025, and Lutnick identifies Ken Moelis—not Cantor—as the person who brought USAR to him in November 2025.

### H2 — Commerce/Lutnick selected Cantor for the Jan. 2026 PIPE

**Current status: unresolved, with evidence leaning against a simple selection theory.**

The pre-existing Cantor relationship supplies a non-government explanation for Cantor's selection. The actual Jan. 2026 selection process, competing proposals, and engagement negotiations remain undisclosed.

### H3 — An existing Cantor relationship economically benefited from a government-linked financing condition

**Current status: supported as transaction structure, subject to fee quantification.**

USAR disclosed that at least $500 million of non-federal capital was a condition/milestone associated with definitive federal documentation, and the $1.5 billion Cantor-led PIPE satisfied that capital requirement. The exact Cantor compensation remains unlocated.

### H4 — Lutnick's government-side participation implicated his former-employer restriction

**Current status: concrete unresolved ethics question.**

Lutnick has acknowledged introducing USAR to the CHIPS team. That occurred within the one-year former-employer period. Whether his participation was in the same "particular matter involving specific parties" in which Cantor was a party or represented a party remains the key classification question.
