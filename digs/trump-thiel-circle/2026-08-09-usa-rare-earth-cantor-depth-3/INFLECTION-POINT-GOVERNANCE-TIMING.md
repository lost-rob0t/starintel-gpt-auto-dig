# Inflection Point governance timing — January 2026

**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** active research

## Question

When and how did control over Inflection Point-managed fund securities change immediately before the January 2026 USA Rare Earth PIPE, and does the public record identify the third member of the three-person investment committee used by Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP?

## Bottom line

The SEC record shows a real governance change effective **January 1, 2026**.

Before that date, 2025 filings described **Michael Blitzer as controlling Inflection Point Asset Management LLC, Inflection Point GP I LLC and Inflection Point Fund I, LP, including voting and investment discretion** over Fund I securities.

On January 5, 2026, Blitzer filed Forms 4 for multiple Inflection Point-related issuers stating that an **administrative change effective January 1, 2026 in the internal governance documents of Inflection Point Asset Management LLC and Inflection Point GP I LLC** changed his beneficial-ownership reporting. The filings expressly state that the change was **not caused by a sale, transfer or other disposition of securities**.

Later SEC filings describe the post-change structure as a three-person investment committee requiring majority approval, consisting of:

1. Michael Blitzer;
2. Kevin Shannon; and
3. an unnamed third individual with no relationship to the relevant issuer or its predecessors/affiliates during the prior three years.

The same three-person description is used for **Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP**, which received the $300.0325 million USAR PIPE allocation.

Separately, the SEC/IAPD firm summary for **Inflection Point Asset Management LLC (CRD 340616 / SEC 802-135401)** says IPAM became an active **Exempt Reporting Adviser** with the SEC effective **January 23, 2026**, three days before the public USAR PIPE/government-financing announcement.

These dates are relevant chronology. They do **not** establish that either the governance amendment or ERA filing was undertaken because of the USAR PIPE.

## Before January 1, 2026

USAR and other Inflection Point-related SEC filings in 2025 repeatedly described Blitzer as controlling:

- Inflection Point Asset Management LLC;
- Inflection Point GP I LLC; and
- Inflection Point Fund I, LP,

including exercise of voting and investment discretion over securities held or to be held by Fund I.

This is the pre-change control baseline.

## January 1, 2026 — administrative governance change

Blitzer's January 5 Forms 4 report January 1 as the transaction/effective date and explain that the filing exists solely because of an **administrative change in internal governance documents** of IPAM and Inflection Point GP I LLC.

The filings expressly say the change did not result from:

- a sale;
- a transfer; or
- another disposition of Fund I securities.

The direct consequence was a change in how Blitzer's beneficial ownership of Fund I-held securities was reported.

## Post-change committee structure

Subsequent SEC prospectuses explain the governance effect more fully: voting and dispositive power over Fund I securities became vested in a **three-member investment committee** and decisions required majority approval.

The filings name Blitzer and Shannon but describe the third member only as a person without a relationship to the issuer or its predecessors/affiliates during the preceding three years.

The filings invoke the so-called **rule of three** as the reason individual committee members are not automatically treated as beneficial owners based solely on committee membership.

## Same structure used for Freedom Fund

USAR's February 2026 S-1 applies materially identical language to **Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP**:

- Inflection Point Freedom Fund GP LLC = general partner;
- Inflection Point Asset Management LLC = investment manager;
- voting/dispositive power = three-person investment committee;
- named members = Blitzer + Shannon;
- third member = unnamed, no prior-three-year USAR relationship.

This supports that Freedom Fund's governance was part of a broader Inflection Point control architecture rather than a uniquely disclosed USAR-only committee.

It does not establish when the Freedom Fund GP/LP documents themselves were adopted.

## January 23, 2026 — IPAM becomes active SEC exempt-reporting adviser

The SEC Investment Adviser Public Disclosure system identifies:

- firm: Inflection Point Asset Management LLC;
- CRD: 340616;
- SEC number: 802-135401;
- status: Exempt Reporting Adviser — Active;
- effective date: **January 23, 2026**.

An ERA files Form ADV reports but is exempt from full investment-adviser registration under the applicable statutory/rule framework.

### What this does not establish

The public firm-summary page does not, by itself, establish:

- that Freedom Fund caused the ERA filing;
- the Freedom Fund formation date;
- the fund's gross asset value;
- the LP roster;
- the GP ownership split; or
- the identity of the third investment-committee member.

The underlying Form ADV/private-fund schedule is therefore a high-priority primary document.

## Candidate third-member analysis

Current public Inflection Point personnel include or have included Michael Blitzer, Kevin Shannon, Adam Saks and other SPAC directors/advisers.

**No candidate is promoted to the third-member identity without an explicit source.**

Adam Saks is a relevant lead because SEC filings say he became IPAM CFO in January 2026, contemporaneous with the governance change. However, no reviewed SEC filing identifies Saks as the third investment-committee member. Treating him as that member would currently be inference only.

Likewise, later personnel such as Dylan Chan cannot be used as evidence for the January committee absent source-backed appointment timing and committee documentation.

## Strongest current inference

The January 1 governance change appears to have shifted voting/dispositive authority from a structure in which Blitzer was publicly described as controlling Fund I to a majority-controlled three-person investment committee used across Inflection Point-managed positions.

That is a **governance fact plus inference about effect**, not evidence of evasion or misconduct.

The timing makes the internal documents worth obtaining because the USAR PIPE engagement and investor process followed within weeks.
