# Inflection Point governance timing — January 2026

**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** active research

## Question

When and how did voting/dispositive authority over Inflection Point-managed fund securities change immediately before the January 2026 USA Rare Earth PIPE, and does the public record identify the third member of the three-person investment committee used by Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP?

## Corrected bottom line

The SEC record shows a real governance change effective **January 1, 2026**, but it should be described narrowly.

Before that date, 2025 filings described **Michael Blitzer as controlling Inflection Point Asset Management LLC (IPAM)** and exercising or sharing voting/investment discretion over securities held through Inflection Point structures.

On January 5, 2026, Blitzer filed Forms 4 for multiple Inflection Point-related issuers stating that an **administrative change effective January 1, 2026 in internal governance documents of IPAM and Inflection Point GP I LLC** changed his beneficial-ownership reporting. The filings expressly state that the change was **not caused by a sale, transfer or other disposition of securities**.

Subsequent issuer filings describe voting/dispositive power over **Inflection Point Fund I securities** as vested in a three-person investment committee requiring majority approval:

1. Michael Blitzer;
2. Kevin Shannon; and
3. an unnamed third individual with no relationship to the relevant issuer or its predecessors/affiliates during the prior three years.

The same committee pattern is used for **Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP**, which received the $300.0325 million USAR PIPE allocation.

**Correction:** the January 1 change does not establish that Blitzer ceased controlling IPAM as a company. March-May 2026 SEC filings for other Inflection Point vehicles still state that **Michael Blitzer controls IPAM** and signs as its Chief Investment Officer. The safer classification is:

> **fund-level voting/dispositive authority was restructured into a majority committee while Blitzer remained a control person of IPAM in other entity-level SEC disclosures.**

Separately, SEC/IAPD identifies **Inflection Point Asset Management LLC (CRD 340616 / SEC 802-135401)** as an active **Exempt Reporting Adviser** effective **January 23, 2026**, three days before the public USAR PIPE/government-financing announcement.

These dates are relevant chronology. They do **not** establish that the governance amendment or ERA filing was undertaken because of the USAR PIPE.

## Before January 1, 2026

2025 SEC filings repeatedly describe Blitzer as controlling IPAM and sharing voting/investment discretion over securities held through Inflection Point sponsor/management entities.

For Fund I and affiliated structures, those disclosures supplied a pre-change beneficial-ownership/control baseline in which Blitzer was personally reported as sharing control over relevant securities.

## January 1, 2026 — administrative governance change

Blitzer's January 5 Forms 4 report January 1 as the transaction/effective date and explain that the filing exists solely because of an **administrative change in internal governance documents** of IPAM and Inflection Point GP I LLC.

The filings expressly say the change did not result from:

- a sale;
- a transfer; or
- another disposition of the securities.

The direct observable consequence was a change in Blitzer's deemed beneficial-ownership reporting for fund-held securities.

## Post-change fund-level committee structure

Subsequent SEC prospectuses explain the governance effect more fully: voting and dispositive power over Fund I securities is vested in a **three-member investment committee**, with decisions requiring majority approval.

The filings name Blitzer and Shannon but describe the third member only as a person without a relationship to the relevant issuer or its predecessors/affiliates during the preceding three years.

The filings invoke the so-called **rule of three** as the reason individual committee members are not automatically treated as beneficial owners based solely on committee membership.

### Company-level IPAM control remains separately disclosed

This committee structure must not be generalized into a claim that Blitzer relinquished control of IPAM itself.

Later SEC filings for Inflection Point Acquisition Corp. VI state that:

- IPAM manages Inflection Point Holdings VI LLC;
- Michael Blitzer **controls IPAM**;
- IPAM and Blitzer share voting/investment discretion over that sponsor vehicle's securities; and
- Blitzer signs filings as IPAM's Chief Investment Officer.

This is compatible with a governance structure in which Blitzer controls the adviser entity while a separate committee governs voting/dispositive decisions for specified fund positions.

## Same committee pattern used for Freedom Fund

USAR's February 2026 S-1 applies materially identical language to **Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP**:

- Inflection Point Freedom Fund GP LLC = general partner;
- Inflection Point Asset Management LLC = investment manager;
- voting/dispositive power over Freedom Fund's USAR shares = three-person investment committee;
- named members = Blitzer + Shannon;
- third member = unnamed, no prior-three-year USAR relationship.

The same named/unnamed formulation also appears in filings for other Inflection Point-associated issuers. That cross-issuer repetition suggests the omission of the third member is a consistent public-disclosure pattern rather than a USAR-only redaction.

It does not establish when the Freedom Fund GP/LP documents themselves were adopted or whether its committee membership was identical at every point in time.

## January 23, 2026 — IPAM becomes active SEC exempt-reporting adviser

The SEC Investment Adviser Public Disclosure system identifies:

- firm: Inflection Point Asset Management LLC;
- CRD: 340616;
- SEC number: 802-135401;
- status: Exempt Reporting Adviser — Active;
- effective date: **January 23, 2026**.

An ERA files Form ADV reports but is exempt from full investment-adviser registration under the applicable statutory/rule framework.

### What the IAPD summary does not establish

The public firm-summary page does not, by itself, establish:

- that Freedom Fund caused the ERA filing;
- the Freedom Fund formation date;
- the fund's gross asset value;
- the LP roster;
- the GP ownership split;
- the full filing/amendment chronology after January 23; or
- the identity of the third investment-committee member.

The underlying Form ADV/private-fund schedules remain high-priority primary documents.

## Third-member search result

The third investment-committee member remains **unidentified in the public SEC material reviewed**.

The same disclosure pattern appears across multiple Inflection Point-associated issuer filings: Blitzer and Shannon are named; the third member is described by independence/no-recent-issuer-relationship criteria but not named.

### Candidate discipline

Current public Inflection Point personnel include or have included Michael Blitzer, Kevin Shannon, Adam Saks and other SPAC directors/advisers.

**No candidate is promoted to the third-member identity without an explicit source.**

Adam Saks remains a lead because SEC/company materials say he became IPAM CFO in January 2026, contemporaneous with the governance change. No reviewed SEC filing identifies Saks as the third committee member. Do not graph him as such.

## Adjacent Fund I economics — keep separate from Freedom Fund

A February 2026 SEC proxy/prospectus for another Inflection Point-associated issuer discloses that:

- Michael Blitzer and Kevin Shannon have economic interests in **Inflection Point Fund I**, including performance allocations, management fees and LP interests; and
- Andrew Gundlach and/or affiliates also have Fund I economic interests including performance allocations, management fees and LP interests.

This expands the known economic network around **Fund I**. It does **not** establish that Gundlach was an LP, GP member, committee member or beneficiary of **Freedom Fund**. The two funds must remain separate unless a source explicitly links them.

## Strongest current finding

The January 1 governance change is best classified as a **fund-level decision-authority restructuring**, not a company-level divestiture of control by Blitzer.

The public chronology is:

`2025 Blitzer control disclosures -> Jan. 1 administrative governance amendment -> Jan. 5 beneficial-ownership filings -> Jan. 23 IPAM ERA effective date -> Jan. 26 USAR PIPE/government announcement`

That chronology justifies obtaining the internal governance and Form ADV/private-fund documents. It does not establish causation or misconduct.
