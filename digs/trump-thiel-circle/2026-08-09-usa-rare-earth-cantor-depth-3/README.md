# USA Rare Earth / Commerce / Cantor — depth-3 conflict inquiry

**Dataset:** `trump-thiel-circle`  
**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** active research  

## Research question

Did the Department of Commerce's 2026 USA Rare Earth financing process create a documented financial benefit for Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. while Howard Lutnick served as Commerce Secretary, and if so, what do public records establish about selection, compensation, recusal, communications, and decision-making?

This is an evidence-first conflict-of-interest inquiry. It does **not** classify the matter as insider trading, bribery, corruption, or another offense without evidence establishing the relevant elements.

## Current assessment

The public record establishes a concrete transaction chain:

`Commerce funding process -> non-federal financing condition -> USAR $1.5B PIPE -> Cantor lead placement-agent role`

The chain is economically consequential, but the currently reviewed records do **not** establish that Commerce selected Cantor, directed USAR to Cantor, or that Howard Lutnick personally caused Cantor to receive the mandate.

Two facts make the chronology worth deeper investigation at the same time:

1. Cantor's relationship with the predecessor SPAC and USA Rare Earth predates Howard Lutnick's Commerce service.
2. USAR's formal 2026 placement-agent engagement with Cantor and Moelis was entered during the Commerce negotiation period, and the private financing directly satisfied a stated condition to reach definitive U.S. government documents.

## Confirmed chronology

### 2023 — Cantor relationship predates Commerce service

Cantor served as underwriter for the predecessor SPAC transaction structure. Public SPAC filings also document private-placement warrant and deferred-underwriting-fee economics associated with Cantor.

**Analytic effect:** this weakens a theory that Commerce introduced Cantor to USAR in 2026. It does not resolve whether an existing Cantor relationship benefited from government-created financing conditions or whether Commerce officials influenced later engagement terms.

### August 2024 — USAR business-combination economics

When the predecessor SPAC moved toward the USA Rare Earth business combination, Cantor's deferred compensation was renegotiated. Public filings describe a base component plus a contingent percentage tied to capital raised above a threshold.

**Open question:** whether those legacy economics were paid, modified, waived, or separate from the January 2026 PIPE placement-agent compensation.

### November 2025 — reported Lutnick / USAR meeting

Congressional investigators state that USAR CEO Barbara Humpton met with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick in November 2025 before the January 2026 announced agreement. The investigation seeks the meeting's substance, participants, follow-up communications, and connection to the later financing structure.

**Status:** the existence/context of the meeting is a subject of congressional inquiry; the underlying Commerce meeting records and communications remain a priority source target.

### On or about January 16, 2026 — placement-agent engagement

USAR's SEC-filed transaction documents state that placement-agent engagement letters with Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. and Moelis were dated on or about January 16, 2026.

This occurred ten days before the January 26 public announcement of both the Commerce letter of intent and the $1.5 billion PIPE.

### January 26, 2026 — Commerce LOI and PIPE announced together

Commerce announced a non-binding letter of intent for up to:

- $277 million in direct CHIPS funding; and
- $1.3 billion in a senior secured loan.

USAR simultaneously announced a $1.5 billion PIPE. Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. was lead placement agent and Moelis was co-placement agent.

USAR's SEC presentation identified **raising at least $500 million in net proceeds from non-federal sources** as a milestone/condition associated with reaching definitive U.S.-government financing documents. The $1.5 billion PIPE satisfied that capital requirement.

### January 28-29, 2026 — PIPE closes

USAR closed the private placement of approximately 69.8 million shares at $21.50 per share for approximately $1.5 billion gross proceeds. USAR stated that the financing was anchored by Inflection Point with additional fundamental and strategic investors.

**Open question:** complete beneficial-investor identification where available through public filings and whether any participant has a material Cantor/Lutnick/Commerce connection.

### June 3, 2026 — definitive Commerce agreements

Commerce finalized the USAR transaction for up to $1.6 billion. USAR filed transaction documents with the SEC, including the direct-funding and securities-issuance framework.

### July 7, 2026 — congressional inquiry expands to Cantor and USAR

Senators Chris Van Hollen, Elizabeth Warren, Ron Wyden, and Representative Zoe Lofgren sought information directly from Cantor and USAR after stating that Secretary Lutnick had not answered their earlier questions.

The lawmakers requested information bearing directly on the unresolved issues in this pass, including:

- how and when Cantor was selected;
- the amount and structure of Cantor compensation;
- communications among Cantor, USAR, Commerce, and Howard Lutnick;
- whether Commerce or Lutnick influenced the placement-agent selection;
- the November 2025 meeting and subsequent contacts; and
- potential financial benefit to Cantor while it was led by Lutnick family members.

These are **investigative questions and allegations**, not adjudicated findings.

## What is established vs. not established

### Established

- Commerce and USAR negotiated and then finalized a federal financing package of up to $1.6 billion.
- USAR raised $1.5 billion of private capital during that process.
- Cantor was lead placement agent for that PIPE.
- USAR described a minimum non-federal capital raise as a condition/milestone for definitive government documentation.
- Cantor had a pre-existing relationship with USAR's predecessor SPAC before Howard Lutnick entered Commerce.
- The formal Cantor/Moelis placement-agent engagement was dated on or about January 16, 2026.
- Congress opened and later expanded a conflict-of-interest inquiry into the transaction sequence.

### Not established by the reviewed evidence

- that Commerce selected Cantor;
- that Howard Lutnick ordered, requested, or pressured USAR to retain Cantor;
- the exact Cantor fee for the January 2026 PIPE;
- that Howard Lutnick retained a prohibited financial interest in Cantor;
- that any recusal requirement was violated;
- insider trading, bribery, kickbacks, or criminal corruption;
- that Cantor's fee was funded directly by federal money rather than by USAR financing proceeds/economics.

## Highest-value evidence gaps

1. **Cantor compensation** — obtain the January 2026 placement-agent engagement letters or fee disclosure sufficient to calculate Cantor's compensation separately from Moelis and legal/advisory fees.
2. **Engagement chronology** — identify who initiated the January 2026 Cantor engagement, when negotiations began, and whether Commerce personnel had knowledge of or input into the selection.
3. **Commerce communications** — locate emails, calendars, memoranda, meeting notes, call logs, or other records connecting Commerce/Lutnick to USAR's financing-agent choice.
4. **Ethics/recusal record** — obtain Howard Lutnick's ethics agreement, divestiture/transfer record, recusals, screening arrangements, waivers or authorizations relevant to Cantor and USAR.
5. **November 2025 meeting** — establish attendees, agenda, materials, follow-up tasks, and whether private-capital requirements or placement-agent selection were discussed.
6. **PIPE investors** — enumerate publicly identifiable purchasers and test for Cantor, Lutnick-family, political, or Commerce-adjacent ties without inferring undisclosed ownership.
7. **Legacy Cantor economics** — reconcile 2023-2024 SPAC underwriting/deferred-fee arrangements against the 2026 placement-agent fee to avoid double counting.
8. **Congressional responses** — obtain Cantor and USAR responses to the July 7 requests and any subsequent Commerce response.

## Evidence rule

Separate each record into one of the following:

- confirmed transaction fact;
- official government/company statement;
- congressional allegation or investigative question;
- counterstatement/denial;
- analytic inference;
- unresolved evidence gap.

Do not promote an inference or allegation into a finding without source-backed evidence.
