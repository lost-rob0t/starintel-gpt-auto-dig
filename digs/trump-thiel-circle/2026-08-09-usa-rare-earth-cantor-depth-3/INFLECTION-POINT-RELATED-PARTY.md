# Inflection Point Freedom Fund — related-party PIPE allocation

**Run:** `usa-rare-earth-cantor-depth-3-2026-08-09`  
**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** active research

## Question

What was Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP, who controlled and economically benefited from it, how did it receive the largest allocation in USA Rare Earth's January 2026 PIPE, and what happened to the shares after closing?

## Bottom line

Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP was the **largest single disclosed January 2026 PIPE purchaser**, buying **13,955,000 USAR shares for $300,032,500** at the same $21.50 price as the other PIPE purchasers.

It was also a **disclosed USAR related party**:

- USAR board chairman **Michael Blitzer** sat on the fund's three-person investment committee.
- USAR board adviser **Kevin Shannon** also sat on the investment committee.
- Blitzer and Shannon were each members of the fund's general partner and special limited partner.
- USAR Chief Legal Officer/Secretary **David Kronenfeld** was a limited partner.
- The fund's general partner was entitled to a **performance allocation on fund gains** and a one-time setup fee of approximately **$1.3 million**.

USAR disclosed procedural controls around the transaction: a pricing committee composed solely of disinterested directors approved the PIPE price, terms, and allocations; the audit committee approved Freedom Fund's participation/allocation under the related-party policy before the company entered the Securities Purchase Agreement; and USAR states that Blitzer, Shannon, and Freedom Fund did not negotiate the PIPE transaction documents.

The fund then appears to have functioned at least partly as an **aggregation/distribution vehicle**. USAR's resale registration became effective at **4:30 p.m. ET on February 11, 2026**. The next day, February 12, Kronenfeld received 6,977 shares in a pro-rata distribution from Freedom Fund. By June 2026 registration materials, Freedom Fund itself was reported as beneficially owning only **15,734 shares**, down from 13,955,000.

That is a reduction of **13,939,266 shares, or ~99.887%** of the original allocation. Public filings reviewed so far do not identify every recipient or establish whether the entire reduction was caused by pro-rata distributions rather than some combination of distributions, transfers, or sales.

This is a source-backed **related-party transaction and economic-benefit chain**, but the reviewed evidence does not establish improper allocation, undisclosed self-dealing, or insider trading.

## Entity structure

### Inflection Point Freedom Fund LP

- PIPE purchaser: 13,955,000 shares.
- Purchase price: $21.50/share.
- Aggregate subscription: $300,032,500.
- General partner: **Inflection Point Freedom Fund GP LLC**.
- Investment manager: **Inflection Point Asset Management LLC**.
- Business address disclosed by USAR: 1680 Michigan Ave, Suite 700 #1016, Miami Beach, FL 33139.

### Investment committee

USAR states voting and dispositive power over Freedom Fund's USAR securities was vested in a three-person investment committee:

1. Michael Blitzer — USAR board chairman;
2. Kevin Shannon — USAR board adviser; and
3. a third individual whom USAR says had no relationship with USAR or its predecessors/affiliates during the preceding three years.

USAR invokes the SEC "rule of three" concept: because voting/dispositive decisions require a majority of a multi-member committee, it does not treat any one committee member as the beneficial owner solely on that basis.

**Unresolved:** the public USAR filings reviewed do not name the third investment-committee member.

### General-partner economics

USAR discloses that:

- Blitzer and Shannon are each members of Freedom Fund's **general partner** and **special limited partner**;
- the general partner is entitled to a **performance allocation** on fund gains; and
- the general partner is entitled to a **one-time setup fee of approximately $1.3 million**.

The disclosure does **not** state:

- the GP ownership percentages;
- how the $1.3M setup fee is divided;
- the performance-allocation percentage;
- the carried-interest split among Blitzer, Shannon, or other GP/SLP members;
- whether the setup fee was paid before or after closing; or
- whether the fee was borne pro rata by the fund's LPs.

Those are current evidence gaps.

### Inflection Point Asset Management

Separate SEC filings describe Michael Blitzer as chairman/CEO or chief investment officer of Inflection Point Asset Management and Kevin Shannon as a founder/partner, director, and portfolio manager. Other Inflection Point filings describe the manager's strategy as concentrated SPAC sponsor and PIPE positions focused on the Inflection Point franchise.

For the Freedom Fund's USAR securities, however, the more specific USAR disclosure controls the ownership classification: voting/dispositive power is described as residing in the three-person investment committee.

## Allocation governance

USAR states that Freedom Fund invested on the **same $21.50 terms as other PIPE investors**.

The company also states:

1. a pricing committee of the USAR board composed solely of disinterested directors approved the $21.50 price, other PIPE terms, and investor allocations after a customary private-placement process led by the placement agents;
2. Blitzer, Shannon, and Freedom Fund did not participate in negotiating the PIPE transaction documents; and
3. USAR's audit committee approved Freedom Fund participation/allocation under the related-party transaction policy before execution of the January 26 Securities Purchase Agreement.

### What remains missing

The public disclosure describes the governance controls but does not expose the underlying decisional record. Highest-value documents are:

- pricing-committee minutes;
- audit-committee minutes;
- placement-agent allocation book;
- indications-of-interest / bookbuilding records;
- conflicts memoranda;
- allocation-comparison analysis showing how the $300.0325M Freedom Fund position was sized relative to demand from unaffiliated investors.

## Price context

The PIPE price was $21.50/share.

Public market data show USAR closed at approximately $24.77 on Friday, January 23, before the Monday January 26 announcement, making $21.50 roughly a **13.2% discount to that immediately preceding close**. But the engagement and marketing process began earlier: USAR closed at approximately $17.69 on January 16, the date around which the placement-agent engagement letters were executed.

Accordingly, the phrase "discounted allocation" requires a benchmark and pricing timestamp. The public materials reviewed do not yet identify the exact pricing-committee timestamp or the reference price used to set $21.50.

Do not infer unfair pricing merely from comparison with the January 23 close.

## Post-closing distribution

### February 3 — resale S-1 filed

USAR filed an S-1 registering the PIPE shares for resale. Freedom Fund was listed with the full **13,955,000 shares**.

### February 11, 4:30 p.m. ET — registration effective

The SEC Notice of Effectiveness for Registration No. 333-293172 states that the S-1 became effective on February 11, 2026 at 4:30 p.m.

### February 12 — Kronenfeld receives pro-rata distribution

David Kronenfeld filed a Form 4 reporting acquisition of **6,977 USAR shares** on February 12 in a pro-rata distribution from Freedom Fund, of which he was a limited partner.

The Form 4 reports a value/price of **$21.4992 per share**, which makes the reported 6,977-share amount approximately **$150,000** at that figure. This is consistent with, but does not by itself prove, an approximately $150,000 economic LP position at the PIPE price.

The one-day sequence — resale registration effective February 11, pro-rata LP distribution February 12 — supports an inference that Freedom Fund was designed at least partly to aggregate investor capital and distribute registered USAR shares to LPs once resale registration was available.

That inference should be tested against the fund's limited-partnership agreement and subscription documents.

### By June — fund retains only 15,734 shares

USAR's June registration materials report Freedom Fund beneficially owning only **15,734 shares**.

Compared with the original 13,955,000 allocation:

- reduction: 13,939,266 shares;
- reduction percentage: ~99.887%;
- remaining percentage: ~0.113%.

The public filings reviewed do not enumerate all LP distributions. Therefore, classify the reduction as **shares no longer beneficially owned by the fund**, with one directly documented pro-rata distribution to Kronenfeld—not as proof that all 13.939M shares were distributed rather than sold/transferred through other permitted mechanisms.

## Separate Blitzer post-announcement purchase

Michael Blitzer filed a Form 4 reporting a personal open-market purchase of **100,000 USAR shares on January 29, 2026** at a weighted-average price of approximately **$21.4386**, or approximately $2.144 million in aggregate.

This trade occurred **after** the January 26 public announcement and after the January 28 PIPE closing. On the evidence reviewed, it is not a preannouncement trading event and does not support an insider-trading allegation.

It is preserved because it affects Blitzer's direct economic exposure to USAR immediately after the government/PIPE announcement.

## Current hypotheses

### H1 — Freedom Fund's allocation was hidden from investors

**Rejected by current evidence.** USAR explicitly disclosed the fund, affiliations, GP economics, and related-party approval process in SEC filings.

### H2 — Blitzer or Shannon personally controlled all voting/dispositive decisions over the $300M position

**Not supported as stated.** USAR says the position was governed by a three-person investment committee under a majority-decision structure and invokes the rule-of-three rationale.

### H3 — Blitzer/Shannon had an economic interest in the fund's success

**Supported, but not quantified.** Both are disclosed members of the GP and SLP; the GP receives a performance allocation and ~$1.3M setup fee. Their individual economics are not disclosed.

### H4 — Freedom Fund was an SPV/aggregation vehicle formed specifically or primarily for the USAR PIPE

**Plausible inference, not yet established.** Evidence supporting it includes the concentrated $300M allocation, setup fee, LP structure, immediate post-effectiveness pro-rata distribution, and near-total reduction in fund-held shares by June. Formation documents and the LP agreement are required before promoting this to a finding.

### H5 — The Freedom Fund allocation itself proves insider trading or improper self-dealing

**Not established.** Related-party status, wall-crossing, and economic benefit are real; securities-law or fiduciary misconduct requires additional facts concerning information timing, trading, allocation fairness, disclosures, approvals, or breaches of duty.
