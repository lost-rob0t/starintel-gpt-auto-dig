## Evidence promotion rules

- a parent label in the uploaded table remains an unverified lead
- a Reddit post remains testimony unless independently corroborated
- a complaint remains an allegation
- a settlement remains a resolution, not an admission
- court findings and registry records may promote claims when they directly establish them
