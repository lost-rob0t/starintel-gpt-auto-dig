# Thiel-company employee → WEF enumeration — depth 1

**Dataset:** `wef`  
**Completed depth:** 1  
**Queued depth:** 2  
**Research cutoff:** July 31, 2026

## Standing recursion rule

Every current and future Thiel-network pass must:

1. enumerate the company or organization first;
2. enumerate publicly identifiable personnel at **all levels**, not only founders and executives;
3. include current employees, former employees, contractors, advisers, board members, operating partners, legal/finance/communications staff and recruiters as separately typed roles;
4. test every person against official World Economic Forum surfaces;
5. preserve the exact WEF relationship type instead of flattening everything into “member”;
6. recurse from every confirmed employee-level hit into meetings, councils, initiatives, authored material, partner organizations, government roles and portfolio companies;
7. record inaccessible rosters and negative searches as coverage gaps, never as proof that no tie exists.

This rule is carried forward in the queued investigation target.

## Relationship types tested

- organization partner or community member;
- official WEF person profile;
- Annual Meeting or regional-meeting participant;
- Young Global Leader;
- Global Shaper;
- Global Future Council member;
- Agenda contributor or report contributor;
- WEF employee, former employee or adviser;
- Technology Pioneer company representative;
- initiative, centre, working-group or community participant.

## Depth-1 findings

1. **Palantir has the densest verified employee-level WEF footprint.** It is both a recurring WEF partner organization and a source of named WEF profiles, participants, council members and Agenda contributors.
2. **The connection is not executive-only.** Confirmed hits include institutional affairs, health, commercial leadership, communications and medical roles.
3. **Thiel's investment firms also have employee-level ties.** Jason Camm links Thiel Capital to Young Global Leaders; Brian Behlendorf links Mithril to WEF participation and prior WEF employment; Kevin Harrington links Clarium to a regional WEF meeting.
4. **Founders Fund's official roster provides the first complete all-level baseline.** Only Peter Thiel and historical partner Ken Howery were confirmed in this initial WEF sweep; the remaining 27 current names are unresolved targets.
5. **Valar's eight-person official roster produced no direct WEF hit in the first sweep.** This remains open because official WEF archives are fragmented across profiles, stories, meeting pages and PDFs.

## Boundary

A WEF profile, meeting appearance, council role, authorship or partner-company employment is evidence of access or participation. It does not by itself establish ideological agreement, command, policy causation or covert control.
