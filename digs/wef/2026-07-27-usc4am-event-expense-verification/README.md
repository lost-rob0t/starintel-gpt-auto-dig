## Core correction

The two USC4AM-related trips discussed in Oakland County expense reporting map to real, publicly documented Center events:

- **Milwaukee:** USC4AM held its C-Suite Strategy Series on Supply Chain Transformation at the GE HealthCare Institute and Marquette University on May 15–16, 2023.
- **Texas:** USC4AM says its final 2023 C-Suite Strategy Series on Digital Transformation was hosted in Texas with Flex. The earlier Milwaukee recap dated the Flex event for November 13–14, 2023.

This materially narrows the accountability issue. The public evidence supports that relevant USC4AM events existed. It does **not** independently prove the exact attendee list, business purpose for every charged item, compliance of each reimbursement, or the completeness of the supporting receipts.

An Oakland County economic-development contact page still lists **Sean Carlson** as deputy county executive. The county page is therefore retained as a stale-source conflict, not treated as current employment proof.

## Evidentiary boundary

- Official USC4AM pages establish the events.
- WXYZ attributes the trip amounts and receipt-documentation gaps to county expense records it reviewed.
- No primary county expense packet, approval chain, attendee roster, executive-committee minutes, or reimbursement audit was independently obtained in this pass.
- Event existence does not by itself validate every expense.
- Missing conference attachments do not by themselves establish fraud, corruption, or personal benefit.
