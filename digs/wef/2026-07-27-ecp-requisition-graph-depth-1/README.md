## Finding

The public evidence supports a two-layer recruitment structure:

1. **Umbrella cohort intake**
   - `R4071`: Geneva, Mumbai, Tokyo and Beijing
   - `R4072`: New York and San Francisco
2. **Later role-specific requisitions**
   - `R4203`: Centre for Advanced Manufacturing and Supply Chains, New York
   - `R4211`: Centre for Nature and Climate, Greater China, Beijing
   - `R4214`: Strategy Office, Greater China, Beijing
   - `R4218`: Industrial and Tech Transformation, Greater China, Beijing

The umbrella postings collect functional and thematic preferences. The later pages name specific centres and teams. This is consistent with Reddit reports of separate Workday entries, newly visible roles and temporary team assignments.

## Evidence boundary

Confirmed:

- The six requisitions above are published on WEF's official Workday domain.
- WEF's official selection sequence is resume screening, asynchronous video interview, hiring-manager/team interview, personality or occupational questionnaire, Talent Acquisition interview, then reference and background checks.
- Applicants publicly reported split statuses and new role entries while umbrella applications remained pending.

Not confirmed:

- Workday automatically transferred any particular applicant.
- `Candidate Assessment` or `Application Under Review` has a standardized WEF-specific meaning beyond the visible label.
- A role-specific posting means an original applicant advanced or was rejected.
- Every Fall 2026 requisition has been enumerated.
