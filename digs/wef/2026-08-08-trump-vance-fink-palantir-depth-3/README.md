## Over the Horizon Action: exact Rockbridge-event bridge recovered

A federal financial disclosure collected by ProPublica records **$100,000 in speaking fees** for Robert F. Kennedy Jr. from **“Over the Horizon Action - Rockbridge Fall Summit, Speech 11/7/2024.”** The compensation section repeats the organization/event name and date.

That matters because the corpus already contains canonical organization `starintel:org:over-the-horizon-action`, based on IRS-derived Form 990 data. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer identifies **Over The Horizon Action**, Arlington, Virginia, EIN **88-0696885**, as a 501(c)(4); its FY2024 filing lists Chris Turner, Chris Applegate and Roger Kimball as directors.

The disclosure directly links the exact organization name to a **Rockbridge Fall Summit**. It does not establish legal ownership by Rockbridge, beneficial ownership, or a consolidated financial structure.

## Turnout for America: official FEC filing resolved

The FEC identifies **Turnout for America**, committee **C00883520**, as an active unauthorized independent-expenditure-only Super PAC registered July 17, 2024, with Janna Rutland as treasurer.

The committee's filed FEC Form 1 adds two useful facts:

- item 6 reports **`NONE`** for any connected organization, affiliated committee, joint fundraising representative or leadership-PAC sponsor;
- Chain Bridge Bank is listed as a depository.

The current FEC committee page reports, for the 2025–2026 cycle through June 30, 2026, $2,535,118.31 in receipts and $2,906,916.39 in disbursements.

These are federal committee facts. They do not erase Reuters' separately sourced reporting that a Buskirk-led Super PAC participated in Rockbridge voter-turnout operations; the FEC's `NONE` entry concerns the filing's formal connected/affiliated-organization field.
