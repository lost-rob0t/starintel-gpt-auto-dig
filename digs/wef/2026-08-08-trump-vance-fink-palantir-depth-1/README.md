## Research question

Which named people and organizations create multiple documented paths among WEF, Trump, Vance, Fink, BlackRock and Palantir?

## Strongest resolved bridge: Michael Kratsios

Michael Kratsios now has three independently documented paths into the seed graph:

1. **WEF:** the World Economic Forum named him to its 2020 Young Global Leaders class and currently maintains a WEF person page for him.
2. **Trump administration:** the White House identifies him as Assistant to the President and Director of OSTP; Trump also appointed him co-chair of PCAST with David Sacks.
3. **Thiel network:** the archived first-Trump White House biography states that immediately before entering the White House he was a Principal at Thiel Capital.

This is a direct personnel crossover. It does **not** establish that Peter Thiel, WEF, or any private company directs Kratsios's government decisions.

## David Sacks: WEF event + White House policy + PayPal network

The World Economic Forum lists David Sacks as a public speaker at the 2026 Annual Meeting in a one-on-one session with Marc Benioff. WEF's Davos recap also identifies him as part of the high-level U.S. delegation.

The White House currently identifies Sacks as Special Advisor for AI and Crypto and, together with Kratsios, as a PCAST co-chair. The repository's existing PayPal Mafia packet separately establishes his PayPal operating history and later White House appointment using primary corporate and government records.

The supported edge is therefore **WEF event participation ↔ Trump technology policy ↔ PayPal/Thiel alumni network**. Event participation is not WEF governance or membership.

## Jacob Helberg: Palantir-to-State bridge

Existing canonical StarIntel records already connect Jacob Helberg to Palantir CEO Alex Karp and to his confirmed State Department under-secretary role. The White House's February 2026 India AI summit release additionally places Helberg in the U.S. delegation led by Michael Kratsios.

This creates a source-backed **Palantir ↔ State Department ↔ Kratsios/White House technology-policy** path. No direct WEF role for Helberg was established in this bounded pass.

## Trae Stephens: Palantir ↔ Founders Fund ↔ Anduril

Founders Fund's current biography identifies Trae Stephens as:

- a Founders Fund partner;
- co-founder and executive chairman of Anduril;
- a former early Palantir employee who led intelligence/defense and international-expansion work.

Founders Fund's Peter Thiel biography independently identifies Thiel as a Founders Fund partner and Palantir co-founder/chairman. Stephens therefore forms a direct institutional bridge from Palantir into a Thiel-led venture firm and Anduril. This pass did not establish a direct WEF role for Stephens.

## Joe Lonsdale: Palantir ↔ 8VC ↔ Anduril / Thiel

8VC's current biography identifies Joe Lonsdale as its founder/managing partner, a Palantir founder, and an early Anduril investor. An 8VC first-party essay separately describes Lonsdale as having worked for years with Peter Thiel and identifies Thiel as a Palantir founder/chairman.

This is a documented capital/founder bridge. No direct WEF role for Lonsdale was established in this bounded pass.

## Rockbridge Network: important but evidence class is lower

Reuters reported, based on internal Rockbridge documents and multiple sources, that JD Vance co-founded Rockbridge with Chris Buskirk in 2019 and that Peter Thiel was among its backers. Reuters also reported that Vance appeared to retain no formal relationship with the organization at the time of its 2024 investigation.

Because the strongest source recovered here is Reuters reporting on non-public documents rather than a public Rockbridge charter, donor ledger, or filing that names every relationship, this edge is retained as **high-quality secondary evidence**, not promoted to a primary-source governance record.

## PCAST

The White House announced in March 2026 that Michael Kratsios and David Sacks co-chair PCAST. The first announced members included Marc Andreessen, Sergey Brin, Safra Catz, Michael Dell, Jacob DeWitte, Fred Ehrsam, Larry Ellison, David Friedberg, Jensen Huang, John Martinis, Bob Mumgaard, Lisa Su and Mark Zuckerberg.

No Palantir executive, Trae Stephens or Joe Lonsdale was named in that first announced member set. PCAST is therefore a strong Trump-administration institutional bridge for Kratsios and Sacks, but this pass does not treat it as a Palantir governance surface.

## Bounded conclusions

- **Kratsios is the strongest newly resolved person-level crossover** because his WEF Young Global Leader status, Trump technology-policy authority and prior Thiel Capital role are each directly documented.
- **Sacks is a second direct WEF/White House crossover**, with his PayPal/Thiel-network path already established elsewhere in the corpus.
- **Helberg is the cleanest Palantir-to-current-executive-branch bridge** in this slice.
- **Stephens and Lonsdale resolve the venture/defense-technology side** through Palantir, Founders Fund, Anduril and 8VC.
- **Rockbridge remains relevant but must retain its secondary-source label** until public primary governance/funding records are recovered.
- These overlapping networks establish access, employment, investment, appointments and event participation. They do not by themselves establish common control, secret coordination, policy capture or misconduct.
