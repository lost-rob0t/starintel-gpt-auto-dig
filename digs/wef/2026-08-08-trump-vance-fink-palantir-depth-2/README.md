## Rockbridge: separate the network claim from entity filings

Reuters' 2024 investigation remains the source establishing that the Rockbridge network described to donors included Better Tomorrow, Over the Horizon, Faithful in Action, Firebrand Action, Revitalization Partners and a Buskirk-led super PAC. Reuters also reported that JD Vance and Chris Buskirk launched Rockbridge in 2019 and that Peter Thiel was among its backers.

That reporting can now be cross-checked against independent filing-derived records for several component entities:

- **Better Tomorrow**, EIN `87-2086524`, is represented in IRS-derived Form 990 data as a Virginia 501(c)(4). Its 2024 filing lists Chris Buskirk as President/Director, Roger Kimball as Director/Secretary and Hannah Huemme as Political Director.
- **Firebrand Action**, EIN `92-0533628`, appears in IRS-derived Form 990-EZ data as a Virginia 501(c)(4). Its 2024 filing lists Chris Buskirk as Director/President, James Blair as Director/Secretary and Matthew J. Peterson as Director.
- **The Faithful In Action**, EIN `93-1558726`, appears in IRS-derived Form 990 data as a Wyoming 501(c)(4). Its 2024 filing lists Chris Buskirk as Director/President, Caitlin Collins Eager as Director and Hannah Huemme as Director.
- **Turnout for America**, FEC committee `C00883520`, is an active independent-expenditure-only committee registered July 17, 2024; the FEC lists Janna Rutland as treasurer.

The filings independently establish legal entities, officer roles and committee status. **They do not themselves say these entities are part of Rockbridge.** The Rockbridge-component edge remains attributed to Reuters' review of internal donor materials and sources.

## PCAST: the governing instrument is public

Executive Order 14177 established the current PCAST on January 23, 2025. The order:

- provides for up to 24 members;
- makes the Assistant to the President for Science and Technology and the Special Advisor for AI and Crypto members and co-chairs;
- directs PCAST to meet regularly;
- authorizes it to solicit information from the research community, private sector, universities, national laboratories, state/local/tribal governments, foundations and nonprofits;
- places funding and administrative/technical support with the Department of Energy;
- delegates applicable Federal Advisory Committee Act functions to the Secretary of Energy, except presidential reporting to Congress;
- terminates the council two years after the order unless extended.

On March 25, 2026, the White House named Michael Kratsios and David Sacks as co-chairs and announced the first members: Marc Andreessen, Sergey Brin, Safra Catz, Michael Dell, Jacob DeWitte, Fred Ehrsam, Larry Ellison, David Friedberg, Jensen Huang, John Martinis, Bob Mumgaard, Lisa Su and Mark Zuckerberg.

The announcement said additional members and first-meeting information would follow. A bounded search through August 8, 2026 did not recover a later public first-meeting notice or minutes. This is a **search result**, not proof that no meeting occurred.
