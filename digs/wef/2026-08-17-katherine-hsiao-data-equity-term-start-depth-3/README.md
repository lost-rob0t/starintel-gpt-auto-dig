## Official term evidence

- WEF's `Annual Report 2022-2023`, published 6 September 2023, says eight focused Fourth Industrial Revolution Global Future Councils kicked off their two-year term during the reporting period.
- In the same centre section, WEF enumerates eight Global Future Council focus areas: artificial intelligence, autonomous mobility, **data equity**, metaverse, quantum technology, space, synthetic biology and technology policy.
- This supports the explicit inference that the Data Equity council was one of the eight two-year terms already kicked off by the time the annual report was published.
- WEF's 5 October 2023 meeting preview says the 16-18 October Dubai meeting would be the **first in-person meeting of the council members' two-year term**.
- The October 2023 Data Equity report directly lists Katherine Hsiao among the `Global Future Council on Data Equity 2023-2024` members.
