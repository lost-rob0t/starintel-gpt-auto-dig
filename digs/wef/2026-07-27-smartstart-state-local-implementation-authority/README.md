## Core finding

Three people publicly named in the SmartStart contributor roster currently lead state institutions with direct local delivery authority:

- **Susan Corbin** — Director of the Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity.
- **Sandra Watson** — President and CEO of the Arizona Commerce Authority and senior economic policy adviser to Arizona's governor.
- **Jeff Noel** — Secretary of the Kentucky Cabinet for Economic Development.

Their institutions administer grants, incentives, workforce facilities and intergovernmental partnerships connecting school districts, community colleges, cities, counties, manufacturers and workforce organizations.

## Concrete implementation mechanisms

### Michigan

Michigan LEO's **Skills in Sync** pilot provides up to `$150,000` in seed funding to as many as three regional networks connecting K-12 educators, local manufacturers, postsecondary providers and workforce or community organizations. Awards were scheduled for August 2026.

### Arizona

The Arizona Commerce Authority supplied **$3.2 million** for the Pima Future48 Workforce Accelerator at Pima Community College, coordinated with the City of Tucson, Pima County and local manufacturers.

### Kentucky

Kentucky records show Jeff Noel's cabinet participating in manufacturing projects coordinated with city and county government, utilities, workforce organizations, education partners and regional economic-development councils. The packet uses the proposed 2026 Hopkinsville U.S. Smokeless Tobacco facility as a current example.

## Evidence boundary

These are strong authority and delivery-channel links, but the reviewed record does **not** establish that:

- Skills in Sync is a SmartStart programme;
- Future48 uses SmartStart curriculum or funding;
- a Kentucky manufacturing project implements SmartStart;
- WEF directed a state or local decision;
- any named official approved a SmartStart grant, contract or school selection.

The contributor identities are resolved from distinctive public names and subject-matter alignment. The public roster itself does not provide affiliations or formal membership categories, so the packet retains identity-resolution caveats.
