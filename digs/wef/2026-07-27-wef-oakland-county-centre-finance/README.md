## Core finding

Michigan and Oakland County established a direct public-finance and operating structure for the World Economic Forum's U.S. Centre for Advanced Manufacturing in Troy, Michigan.

The Michigan Strategic Fund approved **Resolution 2022-080** on May 24, 2022:

- **$3 million** grant to Automation Alley;
- three-year term, described as `$1 million` annually;
- approved **9–0**, with no recusals;
- authorized the MSF Fund Manager to negotiate final terms and execute the grant documents;
- required repayment if the grantee breached the agreement or failed to achieve measurable outcomes.

Oakland County publicly announced that it planned to match the state funding with **$1 million annually for three years**. The exact county appropriation, resolution, executed agreement and payment history were not located in this pass, so the county side remains an official public commitment rather than a fully reconciled award record.

## Approved objectives versus completed outcomes

The board packet authorized and anticipated a substantial programme of work, including:

- Michigan-based advanced-manufacturing pilots capable of national scaling;
- government-stakeholder engagement on policy design;
- WEF intelligence and briefing distribution;
- co-branded reports, events and white papers;
- international investment and business-network development;
- a Michigan executive opportunity connected to the WEF Annual Meeting in Davos;
- participation in a global advisory structure;
- three WEF staff resources assigned to content, engagement and platform curation;
- efforts to secure federal political support and leverage additional grants.

These are stored as **approved objectives or anticipated deliverables**, not as proof each item was completed.

## Local implementation precursor

Oakland County previously committed **$10 million in federal COVID-relief funding** to Project DIAMOnD with Automation Alley. The county and centre later described the U.S. Centre as a champion or supporting partner of that distributed-manufacturing network. This establishes a concrete local implementation channel but does not prove that all Project DIAMOnD spending was controlled by WEF.
