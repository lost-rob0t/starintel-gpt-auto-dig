# WEF Data Equity 2023 PDF affiliation closure — depth 3

This pass closes the fixed-roster and publication-era affiliation gap for the 2023 WEF Data Equity paper.

## Artifact recovery

The official WEF publication page still exposes the report and its `www3.weforum.org` PDF endpoint, but that PDF host continued to time out in this research environment. The same WEF-branded report is available through arXiv as `2311.10741`, including the contributor pages.

That artifact is enough to replace profile-based backdating with direct report-dated role evidence.

## Closed findings

- The contributor section explicitly identifies the **Global Future Council on Data Equity 2023-2024**.
- The 22 arXiv-listed authors resolve to **19 council members plus three WEF staff contributors**.
- **Katherine Hsiao** is listed as **Executive Vice-President; Head, Health and Life Sciences, Palantir Technologies**.
- Previously unresolved **David Kanamugire** is listed as **Chief Executive Officer, National Cyber Security Agency of Rwanda**.
- The report directly supplies publication-era affiliations for the rest of the fixed council roster, including Mastercard, DataKind, SDAIA, University of Virginia, Digital Rights Foundation, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, Adecco Group, University of Waikato, Aapti Institute, Fudan University, Helpful Places, Digital Citizenship, Ushahidi, Global Partners Digital, Google and UN OCHA.

The employer labels are preserved as contemporaneous professional affiliations. They are **not** promoted into employer authorship, sponsorship, endorsement or control claims.
