# WEF Data Policy 2020-2021 roster expansion — depth 1

This pass expands the historical Data Policy membership surface and immediately crossmatches only exact identities against the fixed later rosters.

## Historical Data Policy identities now recovered

### Dated person evidence

- **JoAnn Stonier** — a December 2020 MyData conference biography identifies her as Co-Chair of the WEF Global Future Council on Data Policy.
- **Maria-Elena Torres-Padilla** — authoritative biographies date her membership to 2020-2021; Academia Europaea narrows it to October 2020 through September 2021.
- **Sina Fazelpour** — Northeastern University and other academic biographies identify him as the 2020-2021 Council Fellow.
- **Wafa Ben-Hassine** — her professional biography records Data Policy membership in 2020; WEF independently confirms historical membership without dates.
- **Alberto Giovanni Busetto** — an October 2021 Adecco Group publication identifies him as a member of the WEF Global Future Council on Data Policy 2021.

### Direct but not exactly dated in the retrieved source

- **Katherine Hsiao** — WEF directly states that she served on the Data Policy council.
- **Brett Solomon** — WEF directly states that he was a member.
- **Estelle Massé** — Access Now's archived biography states that she was a member; the recovered biography itself does not expose an exact membership interval.

### Organization-level membership

- **Dawex** — WEF and Dawex state that the company joined in 2020 and participated in a two-year programme. This remains an organization edge unless a person-specific seat is separately established.

## Cross-term carryover

Three exact people from the recovered Data Policy surface recur on the fixed 2023-2024 Data Equity roster:

1. JoAnn Stonier
2. Katherine Hsiao
3. Alberto Giovanni Busetto

Among those three, Busetto also appears on WEF's current 2025-2026 Data Frontiers roster, producing the first directly supported three-generation person continuity:

`Data Policy -> Data Equity -> Data Frontiers`

This is a sequence of public council memberships, not proof of uninterrupted service, policy agreement or institutional influence.
