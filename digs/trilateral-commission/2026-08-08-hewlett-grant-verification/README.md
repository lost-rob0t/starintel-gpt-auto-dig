# Trilateral Commission Hewlett grant verification pass — 2026-08-08

## Scope

This is a same-depth donor-side follow-up to the broader funding audit. It verifies the largest project-specific modern funding lead found so far and separates the **verified grant fact** from the **possible downstream program relationship**.

## Finding

### William & Flora Hewlett Foundation → Trilateral Commission North America: $400,000 in 2020

The William & Flora Hewlett Foundation is EIN `94-1655673`. ProPublica's IRS-derived nonprofit index identifies its FY2020 Form 990-PF and lists that return as filed on 2021-11-03.

An IRS-derived FY2020 grant table exposes this grant row:

- donor: William & Flora Hewlett Foundation
- recipient: `TRILATERAL COMMISSION NORTH AMERICA`
- amount: **$400,000**
- year: **2020**
- purpose: **FOR SUPPORT OF THE RECONCEIVING CAPITALISM INITIATIVE**

This is the strongest project-specific modern donor-side funding edge found in the current pass.

### Related 2021 Commission program: strong thematic/timeline match, exact linkage still unresolved

The Trilateral Commission says it convened its `Task Force on Global Capitalism in Transition` in 2021. Its stated topics include climate change, disruption from the digital revolution, rising inequality, and movement toward more sustainable and inclusive capitalism.

The 2020 Hewlett grant purpose and the 2021 Commission task force are therefore **thematically and chronologically consistent**. However, the sources reviewed in this pass do not prove that `Reconceiving Capitalism Initiative` and `Task Force on Global Capitalism in Transition` are the exact same project or that the Hewlett grant funded every part of the later task force.

Do not collapse those two project names into one graph node without direct grant-page, agreement, budget, or Commission documentary evidence.

## Retrieval limitation

The original Hewlett grant-page URL is still referenced by secondary sources but Hewlett's site blocks the current automated fetch path. ProPublica exposes the FY2020 990-PF index and XML link, but the XML download also returned an access error through the current web tool.

Therefore:

- donor identity and FY2020 filing provenance are high confidence;
- the $400,000 grant row is supported by an IRS-dataset-derived grant table;
- exact project-to-task-force linkage remains unresolved.

## Data-model implications

1. Preserve a dated Hewlett Foundation → Trilateral Commission North America funding edge for 2020, amount `$400,000`, purpose `Reconceiving Capitalism Initiative`.
2. Keep `Reconceiving Capitalism Initiative` and `Task Force on Global Capitalism in Transition` separate until direct identity evidence exists.
3. Record any linkage between them as a research target or qualified inference, not a verified relation.
4. Funding does not establish control, coordination, policy capture, or agreement with the recipient's conclusions.
