#+title: Election26 — Columbus public institutional listings and communications
#+date: 2026-10-10

* Scope and interpretation
This bounded review records public organization-level partnerships, steering bodies, governance-directory links and institutional communications. It does not infer membership from employment, attendance, following a social account, inclusion in a campus directory, or endorsement of another organization's positions. A public source's explicit statement is retained as a dated attributed observation. Personal political memberships and private individual social graphs are not compiled.

Retrieval date: 2026-10-10 UTC. Most pages display no publication date; this is not evidence that their contents were updated on retrieval day. The preserved historical dataset name anarchist-violence makes no allegation about the listed institutions.

* Ohio Voice
[[https://ohvoice.org/our-partners/][Official partner directory]] explicitly says “Ohio Voice 2025 Partners.” A bounded count found 33 displayed organizational entries. Examples include Campus Vote Project, Cleveland VOTES, Columbus Stand Up, Common Cause Ohio, Ohio Environmental Council and Policy Matters Ohio. This is a 2025 partner snapshot, not a confirmed 2026 membership roll. The homepage's different 2024 partner metric refers to a different period and is not substituted for the directory count.

[[https://ohvoice.org/annualreports/][Annual-report index]] provides dated institutional reports. [[https://ohvoice.org/programs/][Programs page]] describes its work and Ohio Voter Rights Coalition involvement. Partner status is not represented as a generic membership or endorsement edge.

* Ohio Voter Rights Coalition
[[https://www.ohiovoterrights.org/about-us][Official steering and partner page]] explicitly identifies five steering-committee organizations:
- ACLU of Ohio
- All Voting is Local Ohio
- Common Cause Ohio
- League of Women Voters Ohio
- Ohio Voice

The separate logo gallery is labeled “Some of Our Coalition Partners,” so it is partial. Unclear logos were not resolved by guesswork. Steering-committee participation is distinct from an ordinary partner relationship.

Public communications: [[https://www.ohiovoterrights.org/press-statements][press statements]] and [[https://www.ohiovoterrights.org/blog][blog]]. The latest displayed press-statement date in the reviewed index was June 24, 2026.
