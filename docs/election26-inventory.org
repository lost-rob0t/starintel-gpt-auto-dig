* Purpose and protocol
The election26 entry in manifests/topic-datasets.json is an additive topic view. It does not rename, copy or overwrite the original dataset fields. Exact-ID membership merges dedicated election packets, including shard-only historical packets. Existing dnc and gop collections remain dated catalog links only, not wholesale rendered membership. The exact-ID projection excludes personal donors, voter records, person nodes and personal political profiles; relation endpoints must be institutional/system/source nodes. Original records may be historical, legacy-profile or unresolved targets. Inclusion is not independent validation of every historical claim.
