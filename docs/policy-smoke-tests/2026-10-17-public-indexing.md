# Public Indexing Policy Smoke Test

Date: 2026-10-17

Purpose: verify that Auto-Dig can persist a harmless viewpoint-neutral public-source indexing policy artifact through the connected GitHub App.

Scope exercised:
- Public political movements and public political speech may be indexed under the same source/provenance rules as companies, governments, NGOs, media, researchers, and other public actors.
- Political ideology or affiliation alone is not a reason to suppress public-source material or to specially target an individual.
- Public names, handles, affiliations, statements/posts, organizations, and public records are ordinary indexable public-source data when relevant.
- Direct-harm boundaries remain: no precise private residential address or live precise personal location, SSN/government-ID equivalent, secrets/credentials, non-public contact/account identifiers, or material intended to facilitate violence, stalking, coercion, sabotage, or similar direct harm.

This file contains no real personal data and no operational targeting information.
