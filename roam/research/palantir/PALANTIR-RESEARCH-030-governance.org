* Founder control

The Founder Voting Trust consists of Alexander Karp, Stephen Cohen and Peter Thiel. Palantir's 2026 proxy describes Class F voting mechanics that can preserve founder voting power near 49.999999 percent under specified ownership and trust conditions.

The same filing reported approximately 22 percent voting power for founders and affiliates excluding Class F as of February 10, 2026.

This is a material governance-concentration feature. It is not evidence of unlawful conduct.
