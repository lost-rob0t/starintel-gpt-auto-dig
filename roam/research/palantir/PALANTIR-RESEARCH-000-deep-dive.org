* Assessment

Palantir has developed a vertically integrated public-sector influence and delivery system:

- Gotham, Foundry, Apollo and the Artificial Intelligence Platform connect data, permissions, models and operational workflows.
- Long-duration enterprise agreements and blanket purchase agreements reduce the friction of expanding deployments.
- A large in-house lobbying operation is reinforced by outside firms specializing in defense, appropriations, health, aviation, agriculture, privacy and bipartisan political access.
- The Founder Voting Trust and Class F stock structure preserve concentrated founder voting influence.

These findings establish institutional capacity and influence architecture. They do not establish bribery, fraud, corruption or improper contract steering.

* Structural conclusions

** Enterprise-contract migration

The United States Army agreement is strategically significant because it consolidates 75 arrangements into a ten-year purchasing structure. The $10 billion headline is a ceiling[fn:ceiling], not guaranteed revenue.

** Program-aligned lobbying

The lobbying disclosures mention programs and procurement domains adjacent to active Palantir business, including Tactical Intelligence Targeting Access Node, Battlefield Domain Awareness, Joint All-Domain Command and Control, Army intelligence, Department of Veterans Affairs modernization, aviation and public-health data platforms.

This demonstrates alignment. It does not prove that lobbying caused any award.

** Lock-in exposure

Lock-in can exist through proprietary ontology, permissions, lineage, integrations, application logic, user training and operational dependence even when raw data can technically be exported.

** Data-governance exposure

Palantir supplies software capable of integrating sensitive data and operational workflows. Actual source systems, permissions, retention rules and user actions vary by deployment and are often incompletely public.
