#+title: Palantir Source and Accounting Methodology
#+description: Source hierarchy, confidence treatment and contract/lobbying normalization rules used for the Palantir dataset.
#+filetags: :research:palantir:methodology:provenance:

* Source hierarchy

The pass prioritizes:

1. Securities and Exchange Commission filings
2. Senate Lobbying Disclosure Act[fn:lda] records
3. USAspending and System for Award Management procurement records
4. Department of Defense and Army award notices
5. National Health Service and United Kingdom tender records
6. Government Accountability Office protest decisions
7. corporate statements
8. advocacy sources, clearly labeled by viewpoint

* Normalization rules

- Use the latest amended lobbying filing rather than adding original and amended versions.
- Exclude a disclosed subcontractor from spend totals when the primary registrant already reports the income.
- Keep contract ceiling[fn:ceiling], potential value, current amount, obligation, outlay and revenue distinct.
- Preserve lower confidence for award leads that have not been reconciled to a complete award record.
- Treat former government employment as a relationship indicator, not proof of corruption.
- Preserve the source viewpoint of corporate and advocacy claims.
- Mark analytical inference explicitly.

* Known limits

- Classified scope is not public.
- Commercial customer identities and contract values are often confidential.
- Award recipient profiles can contain aliases and child profiles.
- Second-quarter 2026 lobbying reports can be amended after the cutoff.
- State, local and foreign lobbying regimes are not fully represented.
- Public contract notices often omit data flows, permissions, user actions and operational results.

* Integrity

The manifest contains a SHA-256 hash for every document path. Database validation checks path identity, one-line NDJSON structure, duplicate identifiers and relation endpoint resolution.

* Footnotes and Glossary

[fn:starintel] StarIntel: A document-centered research system that preserves entities, relationships, events, evidence, provenance, confidence and investigative questions as structured records.

[fn:ndjson] Newline-delimited JSON (NDJSON): A text format in which each line is one complete JavaScript Object Notation object. This repository stores one object per file.

[fn:orgroam] Org-roam: An Emacs knowledge-management system that links Org files through stable identifiers and maintains a graph database of nodes and backlinks.

[fn:ceiling] Contract ceiling: The maximum amount that may be ordered under a contract vehicle. It is not the same as money obligated, paid or recognized as revenue.

[fn:lda] Lobbying Disclosure Act filing: A federal disclosure reporting lobbying registrants, clients, lobbyists, issue areas and reported expenses or income.
