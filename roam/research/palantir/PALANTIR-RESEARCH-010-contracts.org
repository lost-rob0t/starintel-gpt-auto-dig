* Accounting rule

Never add headline values until every amount is classified as one of:

- ceiling[fn:ceiling]
- potential value
- current award amount
- obligation
- outlay
- recognized revenue

A single award can expose several of these measures at once. They are not interchangeable.

* Procurement concentration questions

1. How much has actually been obligated and paid under each vehicle?
2. Which award profiles are aliases rather than additive recipients?
3. What migration, termination-assistance and interoperability clauses exist?
4. How many competitions received a single bid?
5. Which installed platforms later became brand-name requirements?
